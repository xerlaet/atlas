# Unified Hand Action Space
---
![Pasted image 20260917203013.png](/attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260917203013.png)
Is it possible for different robotic hands to share an action space? We think so. The results are that you can utilize training data from almost any dataset and convert it into something that your robot can understand, as well as allowing for zero-shot deployment on completely new hardware

Website: https://irvlutd.github.io/UHAS/
Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03570
