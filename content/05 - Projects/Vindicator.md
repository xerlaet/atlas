# Vindicator
---

In this project I learned to solder and wire together custom components. The end result was a neat 1lb undercutter robot that placed first at it's second competition. Ultimately it was limited by the fact that the motor was direct driving the blade.

See it in action here: [youtube](https://youtu.be/WHLbwqPn1DI?t=3990)
