# FTC Ultimate Goal
---

Despite having only a small intake on the back of the robot, it performed really well. We placed 4th in Texas and managed to set an in person world record score of 522. We double qualified for the World Championship, however it was cancelled due to Covid-19.

My favorite feature of the robot was the OpenCV-based auto-aiming system
