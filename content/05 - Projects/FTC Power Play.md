# FTC Power Play
---

The code was essentially just copy paste from previous year at this point, so I went full in on modeling. We had around 8? different designs, and in the end we decided to keep it simple. Still, probably the cleanest looking robot I've designed to date.

![[ironclad-render.png]]

We advanced to state and probably could have gone on to worlds except I made several silly mistakes and the judges calculated our score wrong in the semis. It is what it is though, I'm still happy with our team. And we learned an important lesson - protect the battery!
