# Principle of Double Effect
---

1. The act itself must be good or morally neutral
2. The evil effect cannot be the means to the good effect
3. The intention must be directed only toward the good effect
4. There must be proportionate reason

In summary, doing evil so that good may come is never allowed (ex. killing civilians to secure surrender) but permitting evil as a side effect of a good action is sometimes allowed (ex. self defense the attack probably will die but in not intended)

Ultimately all judgments are flawed due to incomplete information and often rushed decisions and the inability to see all possible futures, we simple should strive to do the best we can at any moment
