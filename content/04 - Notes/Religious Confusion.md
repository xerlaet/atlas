# Religious Confusion
---

If God desires a clear relationship with humanity, He would provide a clear, unambiguous, universally accessible revelation. Instead, humanity is fractured into thousands of mutually exclusive religions, sects, and holy texts, with where you are born being the primary predictor of what you believe. This seems contrary to what we would expect for a form of divine communication.

## Analysis
God has revealed Himself generally to all people, but human sinfulness and cultural corruption have distorted the message. Perhaps He doesn't want to cut through the noise and provide a clear message.

Miracles seem to largely be vindictive of Catholicism. It might be because it has been around for so long and has the expectation and best bureaucratic process of investigating miracles, but it could also be because God is trying to signal Catholicism as being true.
