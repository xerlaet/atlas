# The Problem of Hell and Moral Luck
---

1. A finite judgment time determines an infinite future
2. Such a determination will either be unfair (some people get more chances than others) or will be the same for everyone
3. Therefore God does not exist

Think about if St. Augustine had died before he converted

What grounds counterfactual truths for middle knowledge?

Also do bodily ascensions make sense?

## Response
#### Context
At the moment of death, each person is judged and either enters Heaven (immediately or through purgatory) or Hell (CCC 1022) - at the moment of death the will is now free from distractions and ratifies the direction it has been choosing all along

Later Jesus will come in all His glory for the Last Judgment where everything will be revealed (CCC 1040)

St. Augustine was in a state of mortal sin at one point during his life: "Lord, give me chastity and continence, but not yet!" (source)

God provides sufficient grace towards every single person in this world (source)

#### Answer
God has middle knowledge and will judge using it - although wouldn't there be infinite worlds for both someone would choose God and not choose God?
