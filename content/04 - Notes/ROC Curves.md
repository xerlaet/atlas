# ROC Curves
---

A **receiver operating characteristic** (ROC) curve plots the true positive rate with regard to the false positive rate. The curve is created by varying the threshold at which samples are classified as positive or negative

![TikZ Diagram](/attachments/tikz-4e5ba7dba677cc8e22c0b5168ba940d0.svg)


Random guessing results in the dashed line from (0,0) to (1,1), the model should perform better than that

Simply showing the **area under the curve** (AUC) is another way of presenting the ROC curve
