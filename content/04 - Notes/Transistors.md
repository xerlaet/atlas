# Transistors
---

A **transistor** is a tiny switch that either allows current to flow or doesn't based on a control input

There are two types of transistors, nMOS allows current when the control is one, and pMOS allows current when the signal is zero
![[Pasted image 20250312181357.png]]

## Physical Construction
The gate is made out of polysilicon (used to be metal)
The insulator is made of out silicon dioxide
The source and drain consists of doped silicon (n=negative, p=positive)

Hence the name metal oxide semiconductor
