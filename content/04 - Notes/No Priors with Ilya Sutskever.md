## Early Insights

Early on, it wasn't known what was the best method for developing AI systems.

The goal of OpenAI from the beginning is to make sure that AGI benefits all of humanity.

Initially open sourcing was the way to do it. However they realized, that in order to make substantial progress, a lot of compute is needed, and a non profit wouldn't be able to do that.

## Best Architectures

It is known for humans that while certain parts of the brain are used for certain tasks, like there is a visual cortex and an auditory cortex, those divisions are not required. In cases where someone loses half their brain at an early age, the remaining half of the brain will adapt and redivide their mind and they can still function well as adults. This points to the idea that all you need is some large generic network and it will subdivide itself as it sees fit in order to adapt to whatever information it receives and needs to work with.

## Predicting the Future

It is hard to predict the future, but there is a high probability that AIs will get better and better. As this becomes more and more apparent, alignment will become more and more popular. But as always, what has yet to happen remains to be seen.
