# Alternate names for things
---

## Everything Pill
I heard a youtuber talk about a pill. This pill would make you study better, become physically stronger, live longer, and be more attractive. All you have to do is to take it once a day. Would you take it? What if I told you that it's free or costs you less than $5 a month. Would you take it? What if I told you that instead of a pill, you have to work out for an hour or so. Would you still take it?

[The original explanation I found](https://youtu.be/FkQ4iWloT_s?t=135)

## Mystery Substance
On a related note, what about a substance that has an acquired taste, will make you stupid or more outgoing or angrier if you take a small amount, will destroy your motor skills and logic if you take a large amount, and will kill you if you drink too much. Would you take it? What if I told you that it's called alcohol and it's socially acceptable. Would you still take it?
