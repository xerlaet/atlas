# Cellular Networks
---

The world is partitioned into **cells** each with a **base station** that interfaces with mobile devices in the cell

Currently the hardware is largely 4G and 5G

The major differences between wired internet and cell networks is a different wireless link layer, mobility is prioritized, users have identities in the form of SIM cards and have to subscribe to a cellular provider
