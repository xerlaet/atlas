# Worldviews
---

**Catholic**:  believes in the God and the Catholic Church, and the teachings and revelations of such 
**Agnostic**: someone who believes that nothing can be known or is known about God or anything immaterial; doesn't claim either faith or disbelief in God
**Atheist**: someone who doesn't believe in God (note that this doesn't mention morality, how life started, etc.)

**Atheistic Worldviews**
1. Everything that can exist does (infinite multiverse)
2. Space exists (it has the simplest version of physical laws and axioms necessary)
	1. math + logic works in it
	2. things can be deterministic and random in it
	3. it expands towards infinity in size at a fixed speed
3. Deistic God (powerful enough to create, has a will or deterministically/randomly causes)

**Catholic Worldview**
4. God exists as a necessary being with certain properties
	1. All knowing
	2. All powerful (ability to create)
	3. All loving/good
	4. Has a will
From this, God chooses to create with His will and power because it is better than not creating something, and He makes something that perfectly optimizes His goodness/perfect love. Meaning (syn. purpose) is the fact that everything has a purpose that God gave to them which they aim to achieve. The ultimate goal is to unite a finite creation back with Him at the end of time.

Things that are to be taken on faith are the trinity, the incarnation, transubstantiation in the eucharist, divine revelation through the historicity of Jesus, papal infallibility and the magisterium, heaven/hell/purgatory, resurrection of the dead, efficacy of the sacraments, immaculate conception, assumption of Mary, moral truths
