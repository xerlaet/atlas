## 1: Naomi and Ruth
**Loss and Loyalty**
- Naomi loses her husband and sons in Moab (Ruth 1).
- Ruth, a Moabite, remains loyal: “Your people shall be my people, and your God my God.”
- Ruth chooses faith and covenant over her past.

## 2: Ruth and Boaz
**Providence**
- Ruth gleans in the field of Boaz, a righteous and wealthy man.
- Boaz shows kindness and protection.
- God’s providence works through ordinary events.

## 3: The Kinsman Redeemer
**Redemption**
- Naomi instructs Ruth to approach Boaz.
- Boaz agrees to act as kinsman-redeemer.
- Redemption restores family line and inheritance.

## 4: Restoration and Legacy
**Marriage and Blessing**
- Boaz redeems Ruth and marries her.
- Naomi’s loss is turned into joy.

**Lineage**
- Ruth becomes the great-grandmother of David (Ruth 4:17).
- A Gentile is brought into God’s covenant people.

## Core Theological Takeaways
- God works through **faithfulness in ordinary life**.
- Loyalty and love reflect **true covenant faith**.
- God’s providence is often **quiet but active**.
- Redemption restores what was lost.
- Ruth points to Christ as the **true Redeemer**.
- Inclusion of a Gentile foreshadows salvation for **all nations**.
