## 1-12: Mt. Sinai
**Census**
- Israel is numbered and organized (Num 1).
- Emphasizes order and readiness as God’s people.

**Camp Arrangement**
- Tribes arranged סביב the Tabernacle (Num 2).
- God’s presence is at the center of Israel’s life.

**Priestly Roles**
- Levites set apart for service to the Tabernacle (Num 3–4).
- Worship is structured and sacred.

**Beginning the Journey**
- Israel departs Sinai guided by God (Num 9–10).
- Complaints begin despite God’s provision.

## 13-21: Wilderness of Paran
**Spies & Rebellion**
- Twelve spies sent into Canaan (Num 13).
- Majority fear; people reject God’s promise.
- Lack of trust leads to rebellion.

**40 Years of Wandering**
- God judges unbelief → generation will not enter the land (Num 14).
- Wandering becomes purification and consequence.

**Continued Disobedience**
- Repeated complaints and failures (Num 16–20).
- Even Moses fails (striking the rock).

**Bronze Serpent**
- Serpents sent as judgment; bronze serpent brings healing (Num 21).
- Looking upon it saves → prefigures Christ on the Cross (John 3:14–15).

## 22-36: Plains of Moab
**Balaam**
- Hired to curse Israel but instead blesses them (Num 22–24).
- God’s will cannot be overturned.

**Preparation for the Land**
- New census for the next generation (Num 26).
- Instructions for inheritance and leadership.

**Joshua Appointed**
- Moses commissions Joshua as successor (Num 27).
- Leadership continues God’s plan.

**Final Preparations**
- Laws and boundaries given before entering Canaan (Num 28–36).

## Core Theological Takeaways
- God is **faithful even when His people are not**.
- Unbelief leads to **loss of blessing**, not loss of God’s plan.
- God’s presence remains with His people in the wilderness.
- Obedience and trust are required to enter God’s promises.
- The bronze serpent points to **salvation through Christ lifted up**.
