# Ontology
---

## Categories of Being
- [Concrete Objects](/notes/concrete-objects) - Physical matter, bodies, and machines
- [Abstract Objects](/notes/abstract-objects) - Numbers, sets, and logical truths
- [Properties](/notes/properties) - Characteristics that things "have" (e.g., color, mass)
- [Relations](/notes/relations) - How things "stand to" one another (e.g., "next to," "part of")

## Mereology (Parts & Wholes)
- [Systems](/notes/systems) - Collections of parts acting as a unified whole
- [Abstraction](/notes/abstraction) - The process of removing details to focus on essence
- [Reductionism](/notes/reductionism) - Analyzing complex systems as the sum of their parts

## Applied Ontology (Computer Science)
- [Knowledge Representation](/notes/knowledge-representation) - How to model the world for AI agents
- [Database Design](/notes/database-design) - Structuring entities and their relationships
- [Semantic Web](/notes/semantic-web) - Linking data through formal ontological frameworks
