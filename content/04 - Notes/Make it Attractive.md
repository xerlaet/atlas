# Make it Attractive
---

We have the brains of our ancestors but temptations they never had to face. Habits are modern-day solutions to ancient desires.

Social influence is real. We tend to imitate habits from the close (family and friends), the many (tribe), and the powerful (those with status and prestige). Join a culture where the desired behavior is normal and respected

**Temptation Bundling**: pairing an action you want to do with an action you need to do to make it more attractive
- After HABIT I NEED, I will HABIT I WANT

Allen Carr's Easy Way to Stop Smoking employs the inversion of this second law - make it unattractive. The reasoning is that by stopping smoking you aren't loosing anything useful, and you will improve almost everything good around you

Re-framing associations can reprogram predictions and transform a hard habit into an attractive one
