# Dynamics
---

**Dynamics** is the branch of **Classical Mechanics** that studies the relationship between the motion of objects and the forces that act upon them. While [Kinematics](/notes/kinematics) describes the geometry of motion, dynamics explains the underlying causes by examining mass, force, and momentum.

## Foundation of Dynamics
The entire field rests on three core principles governing inertia, proportional force, and action-reaction pairs. See the full breakdown at [Newton's Laws of Motion](/notes/newtons-laws-of-motion).

## Common Forces & Free Body Diagrams
To solve dynamic systems, all acting vectors are isolated onto a central point using a **Free Body Diagram**. The most common mechanical forces include:
- **Gravity** ($F_g$): The downward pull exerted by a massive body.
- **Normal Force** ($F_N$): The perpendicular reactive force exerted by a surface.
- **Applied Force** ($F_A$): An external push or pull placed upon the object.
- **Friction** ($F_f$): The resistive force opposing motion across a surface.

$$
\begin{aligned}
F_g &= mg \\
F_f &= \mu F_N
\end{aligned}
$$
