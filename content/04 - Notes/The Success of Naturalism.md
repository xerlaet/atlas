# The Success of Naturalism
---

For all of human history, mysteries (lightning, disease, planetary orbits, earthquakes, mental illness) were attributed to supernatural agents. Over time, the scientific method has continuously replaced these with naturalistic explanations.

It is arguable, but the track record of natural replacing the supernatural is close to 100%. This provides strong [Inductive Reasoning](/notes/inductive-reasoning) to believe the remaining mysteries (e.g. origins of life, consciousness) will also have natural explanations.
