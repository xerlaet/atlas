# Work
---

- **Telecom-muting (Telecommuting/Telework):** The phenomenon of working at a distance from the traditional company office or factory, connected in cyberspace, often from an employee's home.
- **Luddites:** A group in the early 1800s who burned weaving looms, fearing that the technology would eliminate their jobs.
- **Job Churn:** The typical process in a flexible economy where a large number of jobs are created and destroyed over a given period, resulting in a net change in employment.
- **OECD (Organization for Economic Co-operation and Development):** An international organization that studied employment trends and concluded that unemployment stems from policies that make economies rigid and hinder adaptation.
- **Offshoring:** The practice of hiring companies or employees in other countries to perform tasks or provide services instead of doing them domestically.
- **Inshoring:** From the perspective of a country receiving work, it refers to foreign companies hiring companies or employees within that country.
- **ECPA (Electronic Communications Privacy Act):** A U.S. law that prohibits interception of email and reading of stored email without a court order, but includes an exception for business systems, allowing employers to read employee email on company systems.
- **Expectation of Privacy:** A legal concept considered by courts when determining whether an employee had a reasonable belief that their actions or communications were private, often influenced by company policy.
- **CFAA (Computer Fraud and Abuse Act):** A U.S. law originally intended for hacking, but which has been controversially applied to prosecute employees for violating employer computer use policies.
