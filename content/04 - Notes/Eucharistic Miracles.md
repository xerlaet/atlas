## Skeptical Response
Almost all of them start because a host was placed in water to dissolve and left alone in a dark humid environment for 7 to 10 days. In control tests by scientists such as Dr. Kelly Kearse, doing this results in red microbial/fungal blooms roughly 15% to 20% of the time.

For the AB blood testing, no Rh factors (+ or -) have been found. While this is because it wasn't tested for, the lack of them fits with bacterial contamination.

I hope more sufficient tests are done (such as proper genomic sequencing) to determine if the samples between Eucharistic miracles are genetically identical (such tests are apparently routine today).
