## 1-18: Exodus from Egypt
**Israel in Egypt**
- Israel multiplies and fills the land (Ex 1).
- Pharaoh fears them → enslaves them and orders death of male children.
- Moses is saved and raised in Pharaoh’s house.

**Call of Moses**
- God appears in the burning bush (Ex 3).
- Reveals His name: *“I AM WHO AM”* (Ex 3:14; CCC 203).
- Sends Moses to free Israel.

**Plagues & Hardness of Heart**
- Pharaoh resists → plagues reveal God’s power over Egypt’s gods.
- Pharaoh hardens his heart, then God confirms him in that choice.

**Passover**
- Lamb sacrificed; blood on doorposts saves from death (Ex 12).
- Israel delivered from judgment.
- Prefigures Christ, the true Paschal Lamb (CCC 608).

**Red Sea**
- God parts the sea → Israel passes through.
- Egypt is defeated.
- Prefigures Baptism (CCC 1221).

**Wilderness Provision**
- God provides manna (bread from heaven) and water (Ex 16–17).
- Prefigures the Eucharist and spiritual sustenance.

## 19-40: Covenant at Sinai
**Covenant at Sinai**
- God establishes Israel as His people (Ex 19).
- A kingdom of priests, a holy nation.

**The Law**
- Ten Commandments + covenant laws (Ex 20–23).
- Moral, social, and worship order (CCC 2056–2063).

**God’s Presence**
- Instructions for the Tabernacle and Ark (Ex 25–31).
- God will dwell among His people.

**Golden Calf**
- Israel breaks the covenant immediately (Ex 32).
- Idolatry = rejection of God despite His salvation.

**Moses’ Intercession**
- Moses mediates for the people.
- God renews the covenant (Ex 34).

**Tabernacle**
- Dwelling place of God’s presence (Ex 35–40).
- God’s glory fills it → but Moses cannot yet fully enter.

## Core Theological Takeaways
- God is **Savior and Deliverer**.
- Salvation = liberation from **slavery to sin**.
- The Passover is fulfilled in **Christ’s sacrifice**.
- The Law reveals how to live in **right relationship with God**.
- God desires to **dwell with His people**.
- Mediation (Moses) points to **Christ the true mediator** (CCC 1544).
