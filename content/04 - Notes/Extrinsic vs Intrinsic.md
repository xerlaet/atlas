# Extrinsic vs Intrinsic
---

Outside vs In

In terms of motivation, extrinsic motivation like money or fame ebbs and flows, and is a main reason why you feel like you never have enough. Intrinsic motivation like a life goal and purpose, Zen like state is much more constant and imperturbable

Chase mastery over money, purpose over position, auto telicity over applause, curiosity over conformity, and autonomy over approval
