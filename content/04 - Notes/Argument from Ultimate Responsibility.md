# Argument from Ultimate Responsibility
---

To be ultimately responsible for a moral action, you must be ultimately responsible for who you are. However, we are not ultimately responsible for ourselves (due to genetics, environment, and being created by God).

## Analysis
Perhaps it doesn't matter that we have certain genetics, and a certain environment.
