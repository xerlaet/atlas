# The Shroud of Turin
---

The shroud first entered the historical record in 1354 in Lirey, France

The **Shroud of Turin Research Project** (STURP) conducted in-depth tests in the late 1970s. One of the findings was that using a VP-8, the shroud produced an anatomically consistent 3D relief (it converts brightness levels as spatial information). The quality or uniqueness of this 3D result is questionable

Looking for pollen on the Shroud is iffy, it has been exposed many times to various environments

The X-ray analysis might prove something - although skeptics talk about the samples being hand picked, the scientists being heavily biased, and the calibration data not aligning with other dating curves, and assumptions about temperature and humidity were made

A carbon-dating measurement by three labs was done in 1988 dating the shroud between 1260 and 1390. Believers have said that the samples could have been medieval patchwork, or heavily contaminated later on

There might be a link between the shroud and the Mandylion of Edessa - however skeptics point to the legendary development of that cloth

Believers point to the fact that no one knows how to reproduce the cloth
