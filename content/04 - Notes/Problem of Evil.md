# Problem of Evil
---

1. An omnipotent, omnibenevolent God would not allow unjustified suffering
2. Unjustified suffering exists
3. Therefore, God does not exist

Here are some examples of suffering that appear to be unjustified:
- **Rowe's Fawn**: lightning strikes a dead tree, causing a forest fire. An innocent fawn is trapped, horribly burned, and lies in agony for several days before dying. No human ever witnesses the event, learns from it, or intervenes
- A five-year-old girl is severely beaten, sexually assaulted, and strangled to death by her mother's boyfriend
- Hundreds of millions of years of animal suffering from starvation, disease, cold, or being eaten alive before humans ever existed
