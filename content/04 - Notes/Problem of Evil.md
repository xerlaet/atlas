# Problem of Evil
---

1. If God exists, all evil is justified
2. Unjustified evil exists (natural disasters, biological predation, Rowe's fawn)
3. Therefore God does not exist

## Response
Answer 1: God allows us to do evil things in order for us to maintain our free will which is more important

Answer 2: God allows evil things to happen which will build character in us

Answer 3: Our world is fallen because of the original fall of the angels

Answer 4: Does evil ultimately matter? God could choose any amount of evil to exist, maybe extra unjustified evil that no one besides the person who experienced it knows it occurs adds to the overall story of the world? It definitely depends on how the story is supposed to end but that is very unclear and a mystery

Perhaps God gave angels the responsibility of being caretakers of evolution, yet the angels said no and let evolution take a brutal path, hence this is just solved by the free will theodicy

Or there were aliens before us who developed in a benevolent way, and they didn't take care of other creatures like us

Although then - if God could forsee would it really be worth it?? Maybe Jesus coming into this world makes it worth it in an infinite sense
