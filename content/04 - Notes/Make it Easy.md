# Make it Easy
---

We are so focused on figuring out the best approach that we never get around to taking action - prioritize action!

Humans naturally follow the law of least effort. Similar to Least Action in physics. Reduce friction with good behaviors, and increase friction with bad behaviors. The environment is important for this

Small habits can impact behavior for minutes or hours afterward - fork in the road

**Two Minute Rule**: When you start a habit, it should take less than two minutes to do. Then once a habit is ritualized, it will be easier to slip into a state of deep focus. Standardize before you optimize.

There are many onetime choices that can automate future habits and deliver increasing rewards over time
