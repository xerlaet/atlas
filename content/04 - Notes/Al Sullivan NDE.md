# Al Sullivan NDE
---

In 1998 Al Sullivan underwent emergency quadruple bypass surgery. He later reported that he floated above and watched the surgical team, specifically describing the unique physical movements and actions by his surgeon, Dr. Takata. Specifically he said that he was "flapping his elbows as if he were trying to fly"

## Skeptical Response
Apparently Sullivan did not immediately log or recount these details immediately during post-op debriefs, they were only heavily recorded and popularized years later. Because of this timing issue, it could very easily have been memory contamination or confabulation.

Also, perhaps he had anesthesia awareness and was able to piece together clues like muffled sounds or touches, knowledge of surgery protocols, or conversational cues from medical staff post-resuscitation.
