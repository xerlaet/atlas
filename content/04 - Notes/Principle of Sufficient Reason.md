# Principle of Sufficient Reason
---

**Principle of Sufficient Reason** (PSR): everything must have a reason, cause, or ground. In other words, there are no "brute facts"

Does quantum mechanics with probabilistic descriptions of how particles behave provide a counter example to PSR?

Does this cause [Modal Collapse](/notes/modal-collapse)? Arguably no because free will can be a non deterministic explanation

Can you selectively apply the PSR to some things for science but then not apply it to the Universe as a whole? Probably not...
