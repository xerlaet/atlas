# Argument from Religious Experience
---

Billions of people have reported feelings of a religious experience in their lives. Whether it be an answer to a prayer, a vision, or something statistically unlikely happening, would you really say that they are all wrong?

## Analysis
Lots of people say they've seen aliens as well, or have had experiences from other religions (e.g. Jesus pointing to Muhammad). How do you evaluate contradictory religious experience claims?

Also humans tend to hallucinate, misremember, lie, and get lucky at lot. Someone always has to win the lottery, and there's a decent chance that person is a believer in some sort of God.

## Response
I don't find this very convincing, I think human beings are just not reliable enough and I think too many people are specifically remembering unlikely coincidences or having rare dreams and passing that off as God's influence in the world. A better version of this is to appeal to [Miracles](/notes/miracles), because then you have more evidence to back up your claim since the aforementioned miracle shouldn't have occurred at all under naturalism.
