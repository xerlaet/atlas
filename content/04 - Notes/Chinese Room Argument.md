# Chinese Room Argument
---

Published in 1980 by philosopher [[John Searle]]

The idea is that that a person finds themselves in a room with a computer program for responding to chinese characters. He knows nothing about Chinese, yet by following a program to manipulate symbols he sends the appropriate strings of Chinese characters back under the door. Those outside would mistakenly suppose there is a Chinese speaker in the room.

The idea is that programming that appears to understand something doesn't actually produce real understanding, so the [[Turing Test]] is inadequate (and it's already been passed many times at this point lol)
