# Forgetting Curve
---

https://ncase.me/remember/

There is a pattern to how you forget information. Hermann Ebbinghaus was the first to do actual experiments by memorizing thousands of nonsense words and recording how much he forgot over time.

![[Pasted image 20250211001509.png]]

You forget most of what you learn in the first 24 hours and the rest decays exponentially if you don't practice recall.

This is similar to muscle growth, you want to train with weights that aren't too easy to lift as you won't get any progress, nor too hard as you won't be able to lift them. You want something just about right that's at the edge of your limits, because that's where growth occurs. Both for muscle, and for memory. And other things too probably.

When making flashcards with facts, facts should be
- small (singular idea)
- connected (pictures, context, personal details, etc.)
- meaningful (learn things that you care about)
