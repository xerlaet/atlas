# Political Parties and Voting
---

The US has a **representative democracy**. The **Voting Rights Act of 1965** established voting as a constitutional right and prohibited literacy tests

Two correlates of voting include income and education

Why vote?
$$
N=P\cdot B-C+D
$$
$N$ = net benefit from voting
$P$ = probability your vote determines the election
$B$ = benefit if your candidate wins
$C$ = cost of voting
$D$ = intrinsic value of voting

Some voters are **apathetic** towards voting believing either that they don't know knowledge about the process or have the will to be involved. Some voters may also feel **alienated** due to feeling that their vote doesn't matter

Since the Constitution doesn't mention elections at all with the exception of campaign finance laws, voting regulations are left up to the states
