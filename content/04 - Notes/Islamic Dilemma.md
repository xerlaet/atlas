# Islamic Dilemma
---

## 1. The Quran's Claims About Itself
* **The Claims:** The text frequently describes itself as "clear" (*Mubeen*), "fully detailed" (*Mufassal*), and "perfected" (*Uhkimat*). 
* **The Caveat (Surah 3:7):** The Quran acknowledges it contains both straightforward/decisive verses (*Muhkamat*) and ambiguous/allegorical verses (*Mutashabihat*) known only to God.

## 2. The Islamic Dilemma
A logical trap posed by critics regarding the Bible:
* **Premise:** The Quran affirms the Torah and Gospel and tells 7th-century Christians to judge by them.
* **Contradiction:** The Bible and Quran fundamentally disagree on core doctrines (e.g., Jesus's crucifixion and divinity).
* **The Trap:** If the Bible is uncorrupted, the Quran is false (for contradicting it). If the Bible is corrupted, the Quran is false (for affirming a corrupted book).

## 3. Muslim Apologetic Defenses
* **Injil vs. Bible:** The Quran affirms the original revelation given *to* Jesus (*Injil*), not the canonized New Testament biographies written *about* him.
* **Tahrif (Corruption):** Muslims argue the modern Bible suffered textual and interpretative corruption over time.
* **Quran as Criterion (*Furqan*):** The Quran serves as the ultimate filter. Whatever in the Bible matches the Quran is original truth; whatever contradicts it is human corruption. 

## 4. The Counter-Critique: The Problem of Ambiguity
* **Criticism:** If the Quran is literally "clear" and "fully detailed," it shouldn't require complex external doctrines (*Tahrif*), Hadith, or scholarly gymnastics to explain its relationship with the Bible.

## 5. Reconciling "Clarity" in Islamic Thought
Different schools of thought reconcile the claim of "clarity" with the need for external interpretation:
* **Orthodox/Mainstream:** Clarity of *purpose*, not an encyclopedia. The Quran provides flawlessly clear general principles (monotheism, salvation), while the Prophet (Hadith) provides the specific practical details.
* **Classical Philosophers & Sufis (Esoteric):** Clarity is multi-layered. The literal outer meaning (*Zahir*) is clear for the masses, while the deeper allegorical meaning (*Batin*) is clear only to the spiritually or philosophically trained.
* **Progressive Islam (Contextual):** "Clear" and "fully detailed" are rhetorical devices. They reflect profound clarity for the 7th-century Arab audience in their specific context, rather than a rigid, literal scientific standard for all time.
