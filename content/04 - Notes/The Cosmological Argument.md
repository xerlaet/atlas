# The Cosmological Argument
---

The Kalam Cosmological Argument:

1. Whatever begins to exist has a cause (principle of sufficient reason)
2. The universe began to exist (probably, but who knows)
3. Therefore the universe had a cause (doesn't have to be God)

Aquinas's first three ways somewhat extend to this as well

## Response
Does the principle of sufficient reason hold (that everything which exists needs a cause to exist)? Not sure

Does the grim reaper paradox hold? It is an interesting setup - one that perhaps could be said to be impossible to exist?
