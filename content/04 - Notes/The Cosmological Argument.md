# The Cosmological Argument
---

The Kalam Cosmological Argument:

1. Whatever begins to exist has a cause (principle of sufficient reason)
2. The universe began to exist (probably)
3. Therefore the universe had a cause (probably God)

Aquinas's first three ways somewhat extend to this as well

## Response
For premise 1, does the principle of sufficient reason hold (that everything which exists needs a cause or reason to exist)? Not sure

For premise 2, does the grim reaper paradox hold? It is an interesting setup - one that perhaps could be said to be impossible to exist?

For premise 3, the cause doesn't have to be God. Who knows if there is another answer for what started everything. Before we had quantum physics or general relativity, no one would have thought that was the case. Maybe it could be something similar for any non material things
