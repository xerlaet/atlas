# Geometry
---

## Euclidean Geometry
1. Any two points can be joined by only one unique line segment
2. Any line segment can be extended infinitely
3. Given a point $p$ and distance $r$, you can draw a circle with center $p$ and radius $r$
4. Any two right angles are congruent
5. Given a line $L$ and point $p$ which is not on $L$, there is only one line parallel to $L$ through $p$
