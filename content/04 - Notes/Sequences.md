# Sequences
---

**Arithmetic sequences** progress with a common difference $d$
$$
\begin{align}
\text{Recursive Formula } a_{n}&=a_{n-1}+d \\
\text{Explicit Formula } a_{n}&=a_{1} + (n-1)d \\
\text{Finite Sum }S_{n}&=\frac{n}{2}(a_{1}+a_{n})
\end{align}
$$

**Geometric sequences** progress with a common ratio $r$
$$
\begin{align}
\text{Recursive Formula } a_{n}&=a_{n-1}\cdot r \\
\text{Explicit Formula } a_{n}&=a_{1}\cdot r^{n-1} \\
\text{Finite Sum }S_{n}&=\frac{a_{1}(1-r^{n})}{1-r}
\end{align}
$$
