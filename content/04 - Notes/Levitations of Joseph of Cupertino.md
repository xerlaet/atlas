# Levitations of Joseph of Cupertino
---

Born into extreme poverty in Cupertino, Italy, Joseph was deeply nonacademic, clumsy, and chronically absent-minded. His life took a dramatic turn when he became a priest.

Beginning around age 25, he would fall into profound mystical trances during prayer or Mass. Reportedly he would rise off the ground, sometimes inches while other times soaring high into the air. Over a 35-year time span they turned into a public spectacle, drawing thousands of curious onlookers.

Eyewitnesses included fellow monks, skeptical inquisitors, hostile church superiors, doctors, and foreign ambassadors.
