# VLANs
---

**Virtual LANs** (VLANs) allow for logical grouping of LANs when the physical grouping doesn't match

**Port-based** VLANs use the ports on the switch to denote what VLAN it is connected to

Connecting routers is done in two primary ways:
1. A **trunk port** is used to carry frames between VLANs over multiple physical switches
2. Connecting via Ethernet sets up a EVPN, and is less cost efficient
