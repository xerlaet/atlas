# The Ontological Argument
---

There are many forms of the argument: [ Ontological Arguments (Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy) ](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/ontological-arguments/)

1. God is the greatest conceivable being
2. It is greater to exist necessarily in reality than merely in the mind
3. Therefore, if God can exist in the mind, He must exist in reality

## Response
Assumes that God is conceivable - also it isn't necessarily the case that the greatest conceivable being will exist in reality

Gödel made a formulation based on "positive properties", however if those positive properties were made into a set there's no reason to believe that it will be theologically interesting, or that there is only one set of such properties
