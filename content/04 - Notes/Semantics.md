# Semantics
---
**Semantics** are the meaning of the expressions, statements, and program units, as opposed to the [[Syntax]] which is the form

"The man saw a boy in the park with a telescope"
```python
print(3/0)
```

The semantic types aren't necessary for understanding a particular language

## Dynamic Semantics
**Operational/Procedural Semantics** is the platform specific description (what does lunch mean)
**Denotational/Functional Semantics** is the mathematical description
