# Marlene Klepees Miracle
---

Marlene Klepees suffered from severe spastic cerebral palsy, which began developing when she was two years old.

By her late teens, she was heavily incapacitated, suffering from severe spasms, seizures, and lost nearly all motor control. She was confined to a wheelchair, and her optic nerves were heavily damaged, leaving her legally blind.

In 1981, she was admitted to the Mayo Clinic in Rochester, Minnesota. After extensive evaluation, a team of top neurologists concluded there was no medical or surgical intervention left to help her as she was rapidly deteriorating.

Klepees claimed she received a vision from God instructing her to leave the hospital and seek prayer. She demanded to be discharged and had someone drive her to a specific church in Iowa, where a pastor who had never met her prayed for her.

She reported feeling a sudden, intense heat. Her limbs relaxed and her eyesight cleared. She then stood up and starting walking and jumping around. Subsequent medical evaluations confirmed she had zero symptoms of cerebral palsy, and her vision was restored to 20/20.

## Skeptical Response
Skeptics argue that this is an example of a Conversion Disorder which was not organic but rather psychological. If the patient undergoes a massive psychological shock, the "disease" can vanish in an instant.

Theists will counter that it's absurd to claim that as a toddler she developed a sophisticated psychological conversion disorder, as well as fooled Mayo Clinic because some specific neurological markers (like abnormal reflexes) are distinct from psychosomatic paralysis. Additionally, her medical records documented structure damage to her optic nerves and eyes, which wouldn't be undamaged psychosomatically.

Theists will also note that being wheelchair-bound and 80 pounds for years will result in severely atrophied muscles. She shouldn't have been able to walk and jump about right away.

On further research it appears she was completely healed after a few weeks so it wasn't entirely immediate. Still, this might be the best miracle healing case yet.
