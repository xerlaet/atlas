# Moral Argument
---

1. If God does not exist, objective moral values and duties do not exist
2. Objective moral values and duties do exist
3. Therefore God exists

## Analysis
How do you defend premise 2? That seems to be the main point of contention

One idea is to draw upon the [Principle of Credulity](/notes/principle-of-credulity) and say that we have a moral intuition that points to an objective morality just as our perception of reality points to an objective reality.

A response to that is that unlike everyone agreeing that a tree exists in a certain location, there have been wild disagreements about morality. Many cultures in the past thought slavery or human sacrifice was the norm and possibly natural or the right thing to do.

The question is, is that analogous to when people thought the earth was flat, or analogous to people preferring different ice cream flavors?

## Stance
I can understand how objective moral values exist, but ethics is just so complicated and controversial at times that I don't think we can be confident about an objective morality out there. If God exists then great we can all be happy about objective morality, but going the other way seems really difficult to me.

Many people have said that subjective morality is unlivable. While I agree is easier to fall into apathy and depression, that's really just a skill issue. I think this is along the same line of reasoning as [Pascal's Wager](/notes/pascals-wager) where it would be better for you if God existed, however believing God exists so that you can be happier about your objective morality is a bad reason to believe in my opinion.
