# The Average Person
---

> Success sets you free. Average imprisons you. If you want to be free, you must do what average people are unwilling to do.

The average American is overweight, spends hours on social media every day, and has less than 1K in savings.

If you're average you have good company, however are you going to be happy with that?

## How to not be average
**Find your fire**, essentially figure out what you care about and what you want to work towards.

**Find you core values**, what you live by

Other people will come and go in your life. The only person you have to live with every day is yourself.

Don't rely on external validation. If you rely on other people to feel good about yourself, you give them the power to make you feel bad about yourself.

Don't stop caring about others, just be free enough to live your life. 

> In three generations everyone who knew you will be dead, including those whose opinions stopped you from doing what you wanted to

**Start by accepting where you are**, then improve from there

Perhaps it isn't best to figure out a goal and work towards it, but instead take steps in a forward direction. Goals can help, but know that they can change. Set small goals not just one big large one. If you go in the right direction, eventually you will get to where you want to be. Or at least as close as you can get.
