# Poor Biological Design
---

Intelligent design implies optimal engineering. Instead, biology displays clumsy, suboptimal architecture (e.g. the laryngeal nerve looping around the aorta, the spine being poorly adapted for bipedalism, the birth canal passing through a narrow pelvis, and a food pipe crossing right over the windpipe).

## Analysis
These flaws might be the result of the Fall (sin degrading creation), or trade-offs in complex systems.
