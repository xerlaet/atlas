# Charisma
---

Some tips:
1. Sprinkle praise
2. Ask about the person's life
3. Find common ground
4. Pre-frame (don't look sad or aggressive)
5. Mimic the other person


**This reminds me of ...** it nicely transitions after a story and can bridge to almost anything

**Ask open ended question** ones that don't invite a one word response
- Why is a great one

**Revival questions** like how do y'all know each other or what's your story

**Be ok with short pauses** perhaps restate what they said last




**Adopt the *tone* and *mannerisms* of the character** this always makes punch lines better

**Mix up the way you speak** as if you're reading a book to a five year old

In order to be funny, it will likely be at the expense of your perfection or convincing another person of something
