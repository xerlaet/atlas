# Transhumanism
---

**Transhumanism** is a movement which advocates for ethical and responsible use to technology to enhance human capabilities overcoming biological limitations

Key ideas include increasing life-span, eradicating disease and suffering, improving cognitive and emotional capabilities, and exploring other potential radical changes. Human nature is a work in progress and wide access is important

Potential negatives such as widening social inequalities and existential risks must be taken seriously

Basic philosophical assumptions should be re-examined with an open mind and critical thinking
