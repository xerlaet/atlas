# Optimal Substructure
---

**Optimal Substructure** is when the optimal solution of the whole problem depends on the optimal solutions of its sub-problems

Both [Dynamic Programming](/notes/dynamic-programming) and [Greedy Algorithms](/notes/greedy-algorithms) require an optimal substructure, the difference is that dynamic programming looks at all the related subproblems to make its choice where the greedy algorithm just chooses whatever looks best in the moment
