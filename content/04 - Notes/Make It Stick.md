# Make It Stick
---

**Learning** is acquiring knowledge and skills to have them readily available from memory when needed

Learning is more durable when it's effortful

## 1. Learning is Misunderstood
Most effective techniques to improve learning:
- Spaced Repetition
- Interleaving topics
- Use testing as a tool to identify areas of weakness
- Elaborate: express new material in your own words and connect it to what you already know

Simply rereading is terrible. It's time consuming, doesn't result in durable memory, and involves a sense of self-deception of fake mastery

## 2. To Learn, Retrieve
Reflection (retrieving knowledge and training, connecting to new experiences, visualizing and rehearsing what to do differently) leads to stronger learning

Our memory follows a forgetting curve which is very steep at the start. Practicing retrieval will make something stick
