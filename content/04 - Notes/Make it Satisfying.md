# Make it Satisfying
---

The first three laws increase the odds a behavior will be performed this time, this law will increase the odds that it will be repeated next time

The brain prioritizes immediate rewards, so find a way to feel successful immediately after completing a habit (such as a check mark on a tracker)

Don't break the chain - a habit tracker can be a visual reminder of progress

Don't miss twice. Anyone can have a bad performance, workout, or day at work. Successful people rebound quickly when they fail

The days that are sluggish and hurt are the ones that really matter

A habit contract will add social cost to behaviors. Knowing that someone else is watching you can be a powerful motivator
