# Types of AI Agents
---

There are four types of agents:
- **Simple Reflex Agents** respond directly to the current percept
- **Model-based Agents** keep track of the world
- **Goal-based Agents** act to achieve goals
- **Utility-based Agents** act to maximize their own expected "happiness"

A **rational agent** acts so as to achieve the best expected outcome. Sometimes these actions are ideally expressed in the form of reflexes or habits that occur without minimal conscious thought and planning

[Rationality is not Perfection](/notes/rationality-is-not-perfection)

The **Standard Model of AI** is to design an agent that optimizes a given, fully specified objective function

**Provably Beneficial Machines** are uncertain about human objectives and act more cautiously. This might solve the [Value Alignment Problem](/notes/value-alignment-problem)
