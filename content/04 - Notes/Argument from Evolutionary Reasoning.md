# Argument from Evolutionary Reasoning
---

1. Evolution does not select for truth seeking abilities
2. If guided natural selection without God is true, we have no reason to trust our senses
3. Therefore we can't trust ourselves if God doesn't exist
4. Since we trust ourselves, God exists

## Response
We assume logic and reasoning at the start in order to get anywhere

Yes perhaps without God these assumptions aren't fully backed up - however do they need to be if they are already assumed?

Also it would seem that a good deal of truth would be obtained from observation of the environment through our senses, and in some respect perhaps we shouldn't always trust ourselves
