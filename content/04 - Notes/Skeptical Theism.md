# Skeptical Theism
---

An omniscient, omnipotent, and omnibenevolent God exists. However, our human minds are limited and cannot fully grasp divine plans. So we shouldn't make any conclusions about God's actions because we can't fully understand them.

This is a drastic position to take, it somewhat counters the [Fine-Tuning of the Universe](/notes/fine-tuning-of-the-universe) and the [Problem of Evil](/notes/problem-of-evil) at the same time.
