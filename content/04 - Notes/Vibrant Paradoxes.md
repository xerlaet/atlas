# Vibrant Paradoxes
---

## Sin and Mercy
Jesus is descended from Jacob, a man who wrestled with God, Ruth, a foreigner, David, a murderer and adulterer. God joins us in our large dysfunctional family

The Church has extreme demands in regard to morals (sexual practices, heroic virtue) but also extreme mercy (forgiveness and absolution for sins). Catholic morality has both of these extremes

Prospective evangelists should draw on Jesus's example on the road to Emmaus, walk with sinners, open the book, and break and bread

The devil is like a very smart, talented, enterprising, and truly wicked person, raised to a far higher pitch of ontological perfection

Power often corrupts. This is widely apparent in almost all political systems

The ongoings of the Church are not independent from trends in society at large. (e.g. clergy sex abuse)

God is on the side of the scapegoated victim (Jesus)

> I see the Church as a field hospital after battle

—Pope Francis
