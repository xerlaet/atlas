# Zeigarnik Effect
---

A task is easier to complete when it is left incomplete. So if a task is deliberately started and left in an incomplete task, it will improve motivation and decrease friction towards starting it.

One of the win conditions to overcoming procrastination is to starting tasks, and the effect of leaving them incomplete will increase the motivation and desire for finishing them.

Once again, just like the Pareto Principle this can be applied recursively, i.e. getting started on getting started

Similarly, you can make it harder to get started on tasks that you don't want to do, i.e. unplugging your monitor from your gaming pc and plugging it into your work pc. Overall, modifying your environment helps.
