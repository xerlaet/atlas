# Small Talk
---

## Random Tips

- paraphrase what the other person said before responding
- view small talk as a game to keep a ball up, but not something like hot potato
- use the environment around you to point something out or start a conversation
- end a conversation with the white flag approach: say you have to go soon, but ask one more question

## Importance

Small talk is literally everywhere, whenever you meet someone new or you are hanging out with a friend or someone you know. Being good at it can make an awkward situation not awkward and even fun.
