# Single Link Control
---

Steiner's Theorem
$$
I_{q}=I_{b}+mm(q^{T}qI-qq^{T})
$$

$$
m_{bz}=I_{zz}\ddot{\theta }
$$

Torque applied by joint $\tau$
Gravity torque = $m_{a}=r_{a}\times f_{a}$,
$\tau_{g}=-mgr\cos \theta$
Friction torque = $\tau_{f}=-b\dot{\theta}$


Dynamics
$$
\tau =M\ddot{\theta }+\mathbf{m} mgr\cos \theta +b\dot{\theta }
$$

$$
\tau =M\ddot{\theta }+h(\theta ,\dot{\theta })
$$
