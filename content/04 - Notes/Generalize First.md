# Generalize First
---
[TED Video about generalizing first](https://youtu.be/BQ2_BwqcFsc)

This video talks about how the 10,000 hour rule might be overused in our society today. We have a tendency to specialize early and focus on what we want to do in the future so that we can get there as soon as we can. However, not having a general foundational knowledge base to start with can hurt you in the long run. Roger Federer, for example, tried out a bunch of different sports from rugby to skateboarding to swimming, but everyone knows him as a famous tennis player.
