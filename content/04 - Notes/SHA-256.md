# SHA-256
---

A **cryptographic hash function** in the SHA-2 family that produces a 256-bit (32-byte) digest from arbitrary-length input

## Algorithm
$$
H = \operatorname{SHA256}(M)
$$
- $M$ = Input message
- $H$ = 256-bit hash digest

## Security
- No practical collision attacks are currently known against full SHA-256
- Used in digital signatures, certificates, and data integrity verification
- Hashing alone does not provide authenticity; use a MAC or digital signature when verification against an attacker is required
