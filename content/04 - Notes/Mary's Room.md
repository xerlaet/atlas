# Mary's Room
---

Mary is a brilliant neuroscientist who has lived her entire life in a black-and-white room, only interacting with the world through a black-and-white monitor. She studies the science of vision perfectly. She knows what wavelengths of light correspond to the color red, exactly how the retina absorbs those photons, and exactly which neurons fire in the brain when a person looks at an apple. In short she possesses every single physical fact about the color red.

One day, the door opens, and Mary walks outside and sees a red apple for the very first time.

Did Mary learn something new? Most philosophers say yes, that she gained the qualia of what it is like to see red.
