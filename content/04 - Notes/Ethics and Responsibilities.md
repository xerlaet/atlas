# Ethics and Responsibilities
---

## Key Terms
- **Computer Ethics:** A broad term that can include social and political issues related to the impact of computers.
- **Professional Ethics:** Ethical issues encountered by individuals within their specific professional roles, focusing on responsibilities to customers, clients, coworkers, employers, users, and the public.
- **Honesty:** A fundamental ethical value involving truthfulness and integrity.
- **Stakeholders:** Individuals or organizations affected by a decision or action.
- **Informed Consent:** The principle of ensuring that individuals understand the implications of an action or agreement before giving their permission.
- **Ethically Obligatory:** An action that is morally required.
- **Ethically Prohibited:** An action that is morally forbidden.
- **Ethically Acceptable:** An action that is morally permissible, though not necessarily the only or best option.
- **Expert System (Decision System):** A computer system that uses models and heuristics incorporating expert knowledge to guide decision making.
- **Whistleblowing:** The act of reporting suspected misconduct or dangers within an organization to people or authorities outside the organization.
- **Conflict of Interest:** A situation in which a person is in a position to benefit personally from actions or decisions made in their official capacity.
- **Kickback:** A payment or gift offered in exchange for a favorable decision or recommendation.
- **Safety-Critical Application:** A system whose failure could result in death, injury, significant property damage, or environmental harm.
- **Specifications:** Documents outlining the requirements, design, and features of a system or software.
