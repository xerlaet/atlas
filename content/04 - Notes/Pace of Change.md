## Change and Developments
The microchip is analogous to the printing press with its far reaching consequences everywhere. 

We have near instant connectivity around the globe (even from the air), massive storage capacity, and computers everywhere

We shouldn't take all that we have for granted, but instead recognize how to do good with the technology that we have. There is a massive amount of "free stuff" in the form of software across the web with various motivations for it being there.

## Ethics
There's a large discussion about ethical theories and what makes the most sense. Generally most people agree about a lot of it, but the basis is unclear (objective morality from God vs subjective), it really depends on how everything is defined

Locke proposed **social contract theory** which is the idea that people submit to the common law for social order

Deontological is act based, Consequentialist is consequence based

A **negative right**, or liberty, is a right to act without interference
A **positive right**, or claim right, is an obligation on some people to provide in a certain way

In summary, human behavior is very complex and there are many trade offs to consider
