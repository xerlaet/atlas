# Agile Development
---

The system is developed as a series of incremental versions with frequent deliveries

It allows for increased customer involvement, incremental delivery, easy embrace to change, and maintaining simplicity

**Extreme Programming** is a method of development that involves rapid iteration several times per day with increments delivered to customers every two weeks
- user requirements are expressed as stores, and each story is then broken down into tasks to complete

Scrum programming is centered around a set of springs that is a similar framework
