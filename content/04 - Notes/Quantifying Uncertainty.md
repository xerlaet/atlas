# Quantifying Uncertainty
---

How to handle uncertainty? (due to partial observability, nondeterminism, adversaries, a general lack of knowledge...)

One idea was with [[Belief States]], where the agent keeps track of all possible worlds and generates contingency plans for each of them. While this can work the problem grows extensively large and sometimes the goal can't be achieved

As opposed to [[Logical Agents]], [[Probabilistic Agents]] can be a degree of belief between 0 and 1 - like [[Fuzzy Logic]]

All the probabilities mean is a probability based on the knowledge base

An agent should choose an action yielding the highest expected utility - [[Utilitarian]]

[[Probability]]
**Prior** or **Unconditional probabilities** are beliefs in the absence of any other information
**Posterior** or **Conditional probabilities** are beliefs after some evidence has been revealed
