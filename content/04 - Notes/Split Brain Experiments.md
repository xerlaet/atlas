# Split Brain Experiments
---

Split brain experiments occur on patients where the corpus callosum (the fiber bundle connecting the brain halves) was severed, typically as an older method to treat epilepsy. Strange things happened:

Participants who saw an the word apple processed by their left brain, the patient would say that they could see it. If they saw it with their right brain, they would say they see nothing. However their left hand (controlled by the right brain) could draw the apple just fine.

When the right brain was given a command to perform an action (e.g. walk), the patient would stand up and start walking. When the researchers asked why they were walking, the left brain had no idea. Instead of saying "I don't know", it fabricated a rationalization like "I'm going to get a drink of water"

Sometimes the two hemispheres disagreed. A patient might reach into a closet with the right hand to put on a blue shirt, while their left hand would actively fight it, trying to put on a red shirt instead. Reportedly one patent's left hand tried to strike his wife, while his right hand grabbed his own left arm to stop it.
