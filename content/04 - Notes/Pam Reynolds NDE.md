# Pam Reynolds NDE
---

In 1991, Pam Reynolds underwent a dangerous surgery to remove a brain aneurysm.

Her body temperature was lowered to 50°F, her heart and breathing was stopped, and the blood was completely drained from her head. Her eyes were taped shut, and molded speakers were placed deep in her ears blasting 100-decibel clicks to ensure her brain stem was unresponsive.

After the surgery, she reported floating above the operating table. She described the bone saw used to open her skull noting that it looked like an electric toothbrush and that bits were kept in a specific custom case. She also recalled a conversation between surgeons, specifically a female voice commentating that her blood vessels were unusually small.

## Skeptical Response
Based on the timing of the medical logs, the saw cutting her skull happened at the beginning of the surgery when she was only under general anesthesia so it could have been anesthesia awareness. Nothing that she reported was necessarily during the time when her brain activity was stopped.

There are pauses between clicks where sound could have gotten to her ears. Also a drill operating directly on the skull could create sound through vibrations.

After the procedure she was unconscious for a long period and reported her experience some time later. This would be ample time for her brain to weave together fragmented sensations from anesthesia awareness to create an out-of-body experience.
