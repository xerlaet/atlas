# Fine-Tuning of the Universe
---

The fundamental constants and quantities of the Universe

1. The constants of the Universe are finely tuned for Life
2. This fine tuning is more probable under Theism than naturalism
3. Theism is more probable than naturalism

## Response
If there's a multiverse, then by the **anthropic principle** we live in one of the universes where conscious life is possible, if not necessary.

Some formulations of a multiverse theory of everything:
- everything possible exists
- every consistent mathematical structure describes a universe
- every logically consistent universe exists

Additionally, while fine-tuning may be necessary for the life that we know of on our planet, perhaps other forms of life can exist in physical structures that we wouldn't think it could. At the very least, God would be able to make that work - to such a level that our conscious selves could be attached to singular electrons

Finally, future developments in science might change our understanding - perhaps a lot of constants are really just a few or have to be a certain way. If it turns out that there is more fine tuning then we think, the response doesn't change. If there is less, then the response gets stronger.

## The Puddle Analogy
Just like a puddle adapts to the size of the hole, life adapts to the universe it's in

This doesn't work because while a puddle can adapt to any size of hole, our life can't adapt to many lifeless worlds. A better analogy is a key perfectly fitting a lock

## Anthropic Principle
We wouldn't be able to observe a universe that we couldn't live in

This doesn't work because we shouldn't exist in the first place given the chance response

## Other life forms
What about other stranger life forms? Like complex gas clouds?

This doesn't work if the universe collapses back on itself after a split second from a negative cosmological constant

## Frequentism
We've only observed one universe. Our sample size is just one

This doesn't work because if the initial conditions spelled out "made by God with love", this objection would imply otherwise. And in science, sometimes we made hypotheses based on one-off events like continental drift or the evolution of humanity. Also, we can simulate (kinda) cosmological simulations and analyze that

## Unjustified assumption
Fine-tuning assumes that the constants could have been otherwise. But how do we know that?

In Bayesian reasoning we distribute possibilities across conceptual possibilities even if they turn out to be metaphysically impossible.
