# Fine-Tuning of the Universe
---

The fundamental constants and quantities of the Universe

1. The constants of the Universe are finely tuned for Life
2. This fine tuning is more probable under Theism than naturalism
3. Theism is more probable than naturalism

## Response
If there's a multiverse, then by the **anthropic principle** we live in one of the universes where conscious life is possible, if not necessary.

Some formulations of a multiverse theory of everything:
- everything possible exists
- every consistent mathematical structure describes a universe
- every logically consistent universe exists

Additionally, while fine-tuning may be necessary for the life that we know of on our planet, perhaps other forms of life can exist in physical structures that we wouldn't think it could. At the very least, God would be able to make that work - to such a level that our conscious selves could be attached to singular electrons

Finally, future developments in science might change our understanding - perhaps a lot of constants are really just a few or have to be a certain way. If it turns out that there is more fine tuning then we think, the response doesn't change. If there is less, then the response gets stronger.
