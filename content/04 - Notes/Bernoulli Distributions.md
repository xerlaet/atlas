# Bernoulli Distributions
---

A **Bernoulli distribution** models a single trial with exactly two possible outcomes (success or failure)

$$
\begin{align}
p&=\text{probability of success} \\
\text{Support: } x&\in \{ 0,1 \} \\
P(X=x)&= \begin{cases}
p & \text{if }x=1 \\
1-p & \text{if }x=0
\end{cases}\\
E(X)&=p \\
V(X)&=p(1-p)
\end{align}
$$

[Binomial Distributions](/notes/binomial-distributions): The sum of $n$ independent Bernoulli trials
