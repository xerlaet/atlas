# Miracle of Calanda
---

In 1637, a 20-year-old Spanish agricultural worker named Miguel Pellicer was run over by a loaded cart, crushing his right leg.

He was taken to the Hospital of Our Lady of Grace in Zaragoza. The head surgeon, Juan de Estanga, amputated it roughly four fingers below the knee, and the severed leg was buried in the hospital cemetery.

For the next two years, Pellicer survived as a begger outside the Basilica of Our Lady of the Pillar in Zaragoza.

In March 1640, Pellicer returned to his parent's home and went to sleep. Shortly after, his mother entered the room and discovered two feet protruding from beneath his blanket. The restored leg was initially cold and bluish but regained normal color and warmth over the next few days, bearing the same scars and physical marks as his original leg had.

Within months, a formal investigative tribunal was called and several witnesses testified under oath, including the surgeon who amputated the leg, the nurses who cared for Pellicer, and the locals who have given him alms.

## Skeptical Response
One response is that his leg was never actually amputated. Perhaps it was severely fractured, and so Pellicer had to resort to begging but tied up his useless lower leg in an attempt to get more alms. Eventually the bone healed or he grew tired of the deception, and returned to his parents house. Perhaps his parents discovered his untied leg by accident and then he had to claim a miracle.

The main problem with this is the surgeon and nurses who testified under oath. The skeptic has to say that they were bribed by Pellicer perhaps. Also strapping the leg for two and a half years would cause massive problems, even if unstrapping it at night.

Also, several locals and nurses claimed to have seem the visible stump themselves. They would all have to be wrong, bribed, or misremember him while he was in the hospital for several months.

Also though why did it take several days for the leg to regain functionality? It seems suspect to me along with the idea that his regrown leg would still have scars from before.
