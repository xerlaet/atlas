# Serendipity
---

**Serendipity** is the active process of turning unexpected events into positive outcomes, standing in stark contrast to **Zemblanity**, which is inevitable misfortune baked into fragile systems. By treating luck as a process rather than a purely random occurrence, individuals can actively cultivate positive opportunities.

## Cultivating Serendipity
- **See Triggers**: Train your [Mindset](/notes/mindset) to extract meaning from surprising or unexpected information (e.g., asking "what surprised you this week?").
- **Seed Triggers**: Provide rich, multidimensional connection points in conversations to give others the opportunity to connect dots with you.
- **Connect the Dots**: Actively search for the hidden opportunity, lesson, or utility within an unexpected event or failure.
- **Act On It**: Overcome the fear of rejection. Reframe [Risk Management](/notes/risk-management) by realizing that the regret of inaction is often a much greater risk than the action itself.

## Philosophy of the Unexpected
Because the sheer volume of variables in life is infinite, unexpected events are mathematically inevitable. While we cannot control external crises, we retain the ultimate freedom to choose our attitude and response, using the unexpected as a starting point for new growth.
