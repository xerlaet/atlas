# Psychophysical Harmony
---

There is a flawless alignment between physical states and conscious experiences that is statistically miraculous under naturalism.

## Analysis
If brain states and subjective experience are the same thing, it's perfectly expected. However it seems intuitive that you can separate the physical behavior from the conscious feeling. This goes back to the [Hard Problem of Consciousness](/notes/hard-problem-of-consciousness).

Under dualism, there are still a couple things to consider. Wouldn't natural selection select for feeling pain when we touch the stove? Critics would argue that evolution only selects for the physical action not the subjective inner experience. However if the person feels great while touching the stove even with their reflex, then they might end up touching the stove again anyways. Similar to smoking.

## Stance
While I can see a lot of discussion that's possible going back and forth if you go ahead and accept substance dualism, it doesn't really convince me. It seems natural to me that if our mental states didn't map well to our physical states it wouldn't work well for evolution.

On the naturalism side, I also think a similar solution is fine. The natural brain has to make sure the body doesn't touch the hot stove again, and inflicting a feeling of pain seems apt for that. However the long term function of the organism is controlled by the brain, it probably is strongly linked to the conscious states. I might be missing some points here, but I don't think this is a good argument.
