# Packet Delay
---

**Packet Delay** comes from four sources:
1. $d_{proc}$ is processing delay
2. $d_{queue}$ is the queue delay
3. $d_{trans}$ is the transmission delay $\frac{L(bits)}{R\left( bits / \sec \right)}$ (reliability and fairness is important)
4. $d_{prop}$ is the propagation delay $\frac{d (m)}{s (m / s)}$

$$
d_{nodal}=d_{proc}+d_{queue}+d_{trans}+d_{prop}
$$
