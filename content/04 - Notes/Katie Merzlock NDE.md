# Katie Merzlock NDE
---

In 1982 7-year-old Katie Merzlock was found floating face-down in a YMCA swimming pool in Idaho. She had no pulse for 19 minutes and was resuscitated by Dr. Melvin Morse.

After a three-day coma, Dr. Morse asked what she remembered. She claimed that she entered total darkness and went into a tunnel, meeting an angel named "Elizabeth" who escorted her to meet Jesus and the "Heavenly Father". She described seeing her mother cooking roast chicken and rice, her father sitting on the couch quietly, her brother playing with a GI Joe toy, and her sister was combing a Barbie's hair to a rock song (which were later verified).

## Skeptical Response
The information isn't that surprising, it seems pretty standard actually. The versification of the home observations lack rigorous controls, perhaps the memories were retrofitted based on Katie's vague statements. Also the narrative was collected after she woke up in the hospital, perhaps she synthesized fragments of overheard conversations from doctors and her parents, later processing them into a cohesive memory.

As for the tunnel illusion, a lack of blood flow causes peripheral vision to drop out at first leaving only central vision intact which could create the illusion of moving through a tunnel toward a bright light.
