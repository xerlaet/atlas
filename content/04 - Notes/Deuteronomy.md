## 1-11: Moses' Opening Speech
**Call to Faithfulness**
- Moses addresses the new generation before entering the Promised Land.
- Recalls Israel’s history → lessons from past failures.

**The Shema**
- “Hear, O Israel: The Lord our God is one Lord” (Deut 6:4).
- “Love the Lord your God with all your heart, soul, and might” (Deut 6:5).
- Total devotion to God is the foundation of the covenant (CCC 2083).

**Call to Obedience**
- Obedience flows from love, not mere rule-following.
- Warning against forgetting God in prosperity.

## 12-26: Collection of Laws
**Centralized Worship**
- One God → one place of worship (Deut 12).
- Guards against idolatry and division.

**Tithes & Care for the Poor**
- Regular offerings to God.
- Special provision for the poor, widows, and strangers (Deut 14).
- Worship and justice are inseparable.

**Leadership in Israel**
- Guidelines for kings, priests, and judges (Deut 16–18).
- Authority must serve God’s law.

**Moral & Social Laws**
- Justice, charity, and holiness in daily life.
- Covenant shapes all aspects of society.

## 27-34: Moses' Final Speech and Death
**Blessings & Curses**
- Obedience → blessing and life.
- Disobedience → devastation and exile (Deut 28).

**The Choice**
- “I have set before you life and death… choose life” (Deut 30:19).
- Covenant requires a real decision.

**Moses’ Death**
- Moses views the Promised Land but does not enter (Deut 34).
- Joshua succeeds him.

## Core Theological Takeaways
- Love of God is **total and central** to the Christian life.
- Obedience is an expression of **love and covenant fidelity**.
- Worship of the one true God must be **pure and undivided**.
- Care for the poor is essential to **true religion**.
- God sets before man **life and death → real moral choice**.
- Moses prefigures Christ, the **true lawgiver and prophet** (CCC 577).
