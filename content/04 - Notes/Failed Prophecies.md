# Failed Prophecies
---

> Truly I tell you, some who are standing here will not taste death before they see the Son of Man coming in his kingdom
	—Matthew 16:28

Did this not fail to happen?

## Analysis
This refers either to the Transfiguration, the Resurrection, or the destruction of Jerusalem in 70 CE, rather than the literal end of the world.

Is this not an ad-hoc defense? Context is important here
