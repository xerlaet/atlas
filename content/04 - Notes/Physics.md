Physics is the study of how reality works. To begin with, here are the seven SI units which measure basic quantities
$$
\begin{array}{|c|c|c|}
\hline
\text{Symbol} & \text{SI Unit Name} & \text{Quantity} \\
\hline
\text{m} & \text{meter} & \text{Length} \\
\text{kg} & \text{kilogram} & \text{Mass} \\
\text{s} & \text{second} & \text{Time} \\
\text{A} & \text{ampere} & \text{Electric Current} \\
\text{K} & \text{kelvin} & \text{Thermodynamic Temperature} \\
\text{mol} & \text{mole} & \text{Amount of Substance} \\
\text{cd} & \text{candela} & \text{Luminous Intensity} \\
\hline
\end{array}
$$

## Energy

$$
\begin{align}
E_{k}=\frac{1}{2}mv^{2} \\
E_{p}=mg\Delta h=\frac{1}{2}k(\Delta x)^{2} \\
P=F\cdot v=\frac{ dE }{ dt }  \\
I=\int r^{2}\, dm =\sum mr^{2}
\end{align}
$$

## Electromagnetism
$$
\begin{align}
P=I\Delta V \\
\Delta V=IR
\end{align}
$$

## Fluids
$$
\begin{align}
P = \frac{F}{A} &= \text{pressure } \frac{kg}{ms^{2}}\\
\rho &= \text{fluid density } \frac{kg}{m^{3}}\\
g &= \text{gravity } \frac{m}{s^{2}}\\
h&=\text{height } m
\end{align}
$$

Pascal's Law for Hydrostatic pressure  $\Delta P=\rho g\Delta h$
