# Physics
---

**Physics** is the fundamental science studying matter, motion, energy, and force across space and time, quantified using [SI Units](/notes/si-units). 

## Classical Mechanics
- [Kinematics](/notes/kinematics) - Trajectories, velocity, and acceleration
- [Dynamics](/notes/dynamics) - Forces and Newton's laws of motion
- [Energy and Momentum](/notes/energy-and-momentum) - Work, collisions, and conservation laws
- [Fluid Mechanics](/notes/fluid-mechanics) - Pressure, buoyancy, and flow dynamics

## Thermodynamics & Matter
- [Thermodynamics](/notes/thermodynamics) - Heat transfer, temperature, and entropy
- [Statistical Mechanics](/notes/statistical-mechanics) - Macroscopic properties from atomic behavior
- [Material Properties](/notes/material-properties) - Density, elasticity, and states of matter

## Electromagnetism
- [Electrostatics](/notes/electrostatics) - Electric fields, voltage, and stationary charge
- [Electrodynamics](/notes/electrodynamics) - Circuits, current, and magnetic induction
- [Electromagnetic Waves](/notes/electromagnetic-waves) - Maxwell's equations and radiation

## Waves & Optics
- [Acoustics and Waves](/notes/acoustics-and-waves) - Oscillations, resonance, and sound
- [Geometric Optics](/notes/geometric-optics) - Ray behavior, lenses, reflection, and refraction
- [Physical Optics](/notes/physical-optics) - Wave nature of light, diffraction, and interference

## Modern Physics
- [Relativity](/notes/relativity) - Spacetime, gravity, and the speed of light
- [Quantum Mechanics](/notes/quantum-mechanics) - Wave-particle duality and subatomic probability
- [Nuclear and Particle Physics](/notes/nuclear-and-particle-physics) - Radioactivity, atoms, and the Standard Model
- [Astrophysics](/notes/astrophysics) - Cosmology, stellar evolution, and orbital mechanics
