# Problem of Divine Simplicity
---

**Divine Simplicity** states that God is absolutely simple: having no parts, distinctions, or potentiality. Any attributes of His (love, justice, power) are His essence.

Arguably this leads to **modal collapse**: if God's attributes are identical to His essence, and His essence is identical to His existence, everything God does or wills is identical to His necessary existence. That seems to destroy free will.

Additionally, it leads to the **property-identity paradox**: how can distinct concepts like "wrath" and "mercy" be identical to each other and to a simple substance?

## Analysis
Perhaps God's attributes are conceptually distinct to our finite minds, but identical in reality in God.

This solves it, however it renders God as being semantically incomprehensible. Should we be ok with/expect that?
