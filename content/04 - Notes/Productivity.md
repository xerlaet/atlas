# Productivity
---

Productivity is not about being busy, it's about doing the things which get us closer to our goals

## How to be Productive
The [Pareto Principle](/notes/pareto-principle), otherwise known as the 80/20 rule, states that 80 percent of the results can often be achieved with 20 percent of the effort

The [Zeigarnik Effect](/notes/zeigarnik-effect) states that tasks left incomplete and partially started will be easier to continue working on

The [Championship Mentality](/notes/championship-mentality) is recognizing the long term goal and being ok with diminished results in the short term to achieve long term success 

Zoom in to focus on tasks, zoom out when feeling lost to focus on yearly or core goals

- Block certain apps
- Plan ahead
- Single-task
- Either don't listen to music, or listen to more ambient styles
- 2 minute rule
- break tasks down into smaller ones
- batch tasks

## Tips
1. Activate flow when possible
2. Plan for real not ideal
3. Two minute rule
4. Create pre-planned choices to eliminate decision fatigue
