# Linear Algebra
---

Linear Algebra is the study of vectors, matrices, and linear transformations. It has a broad range of applications across mathematics and computer science

[Vectors](/notes/vectors) are elements that have both a magnitude and direction and are basic building blocks of Linear Algebra
- Properties, lengths/angles, span/linear independence and dependence

[Matrices](/notes/matrices) are a collection of vectors and can represent a wide array of information due to their versatility
- Properties LU, CR, QR, XAX^-1, 

[Linear Equations](/notes/linear-equations)
- Ax=b, elimination, inverses, permutations and transpose

[Vector Subspaces](/notes/vector-subspaces)
- column, row, null, left null

[Orthogonality](/notes/orthogonality) is a property between vectors they are perpendicular and for matrices when each column is perpendicular
- projections, least squares, orthonormal bases

[Determinants](/notes/determinants)
- what it means, cofactors, how it changes based on row reductions/swapping rows
- also trace

[Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors](/notes/eigenvalues-and-eigenvectors)
- Ax=Lx, diagonalization
