# Algorithmic Design Paradigms
---

**Algorithmic Design Paradigms** are general strategies for designing algorithms. The best solution will vary based on the problem

[Brute Force](/notes/brute-force) calculates every possibility and then checks them to see what is best or what works

[Divide and Conquer](/notes/divide-and-conquer) breaks a problem into non overlapping subproblems and recursively solves each of them and combines them together

[Dynamic Programming](/notes/dynamic-programming) breaks a problem into overlapping subproblems, stores the solutions, and uses those to calculate the final answer

[Greedy Algorithms](/notes/greedy-algorithms) makes the locally optimal choice at every step
