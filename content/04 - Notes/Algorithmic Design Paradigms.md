# Algorithmic Design Paradigms
---

**Algorithmic Design Paradigms** are general strategies for designing algorithms. The best solution will vary based on the problem

[[Brute Force]] calculates every possibility and then checks them to see what is best or what works

[[Divide and Conquer]] breaks a problem into non overlapping subproblems and recursively solves each of them and combines them together

[[Dynamic Programming]] breaks a problem into overlapping subproblems, stores the solutions, and uses those to calculate the final answer

[[Greedy Algorithms]] makes the locally optimal choice at every step
