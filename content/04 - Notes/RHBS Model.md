# RHBS Model
---

The **RHBS Model** is a potential explanation for the resurrection put forth by some skeptics

![Pasted image 20260309205003.png](/attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260309205003.png)

## 1. Reburial
**Reburial**: Jesus' body was removed from the Tomb before Sunday morning, probably by Joseph of Arimethea

## 2. Hallucinations
**Hallucinations**: following the discovery of the empty tomb, one or more of Jesus' followers experienced individual hallucinations of the risen Jesus

## 3. Group Experiences
**Group Experiences**: precipated by these experiences, early disciples had several collective religious experiences of the risen Jesus

## 4. Cognitive Biases
**Cognitive Biases**: while discussing these experiences, the disciples' memories were reshaped through processes of reconstructive recall, social memory contagion, irrational belief persistence, and cognitive dissonance

## 5. Socialism
**Socialism**: disagreement and doubt was muted by social pressure for conformity
