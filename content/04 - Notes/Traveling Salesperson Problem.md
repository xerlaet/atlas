# Traveling Salesperson Problem
---

The **Traveling Salesperson Problem** (TSP) is a touring problem where every city on a map must be visited. The goal is to find a path with a minimum cost

In other words, it asks for a Hamiltonian circuit with minimal total weight, and the brute force approach has to consider $\frac{(n-1)!}{2}$ different paths. No such algorithm has been found in polynomial time, however efficient algorithms have been found that can give approximate solutions

This problem has a lot of impact in the real world in situations such as bus routes, movements of drills, and movements of stocking machines
