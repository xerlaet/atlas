# Expressions and Assignment Statements
---

**Expressions** are the fundamental means for specifying computations, and depend on the order of operator and operand evaluation

Arithmetic expressions consist of operators, operands, parentheses, and function calls. Design considerations include the precedence, associativity, order of operands, evaluation side effects, operator overloading, and type mixing

Operators may have an **arity** of one (ex. ++), two (ex. +), or three (ex. ?:)
