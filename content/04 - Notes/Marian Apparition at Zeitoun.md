# Marian Apparition at Zeitoun
---

## Summary
There was a series of reported sightings of the Blessed Virgin Mary about the Coptic Orthodox Church of Saint Mary in the Zeitoun district of Cairo, Egypt, from April 2, 1968 intermittently until 1971.

Police and authorities report finding no device within 15 miles that could project such an image

Some reports note that the onlookers didn't always see the same thing, possible explanations could be God allowing only some people to see or it could be due to different vantage points, etc.

There was a growing Muslim Coptic hostility due to scapegoat allegations relating to a recent Six-Day war (less than a year prior), Egypt was in severe economic hardship and national morale was shattered

No photo or video has been formally authenticated from a forensic sense - some are clearly retouched, enhanced, or misrepresented. The best evidence is instead mass eyewitness testimony

The apparitions didn't contribute to any substantial recovery for the country, although it did improve morale

## Possible Explanations
- It was man made
	- The Egyptian government (or someone else) created various lights at night and the crowd interpreted/hallucinated the rest
	- Possible incentive was to drum up tourism in the region to help the economy (although it didn't help much)
- It was a miracle
