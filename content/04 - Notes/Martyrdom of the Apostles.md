# Martyrdom of the Apostles
---

Living memory is up to A.D. ~200

Golden thread of how much belief costs someone

Even still most of the apostles claim to have seen Jesus?

## Highest Probability
- **Peter** - crucified in Rome under Nero
- **Paul** - executed in Rome under Nero
- **James, brother of Jesus** - stoned to death by Jewish authorities in Jerusalem
- **James, son of Zebedee** - executed by Herod Agrippa

## Probable
- **Thomas** - killed in India by a spear
- **Andrew**
