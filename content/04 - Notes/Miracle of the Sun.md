# Miracle of the Sun
---

## Summary
An appearance was foretold by an appearance from Mary to some children for October 13, 1917. 

A large crowd was present of many faiths and there were many reports of something to do with the sun where it moved around.

## Possible Explanations
- Staring that the sun for long periods of time is never a good idea
- People were expecting a miracle
- Keep in mind that the eyewitness testimony was inconsistent and contradictory, and no photographs were taken despite many cameras being present
