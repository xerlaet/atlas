# Hard Problem of Consciousness
---

How does the mind arise from a physical brain? To what extent is knowledge and understanding programmable?

## Response
The problem is aptly named. Something to note though is that even with substance dualism, consciousness still seems really hard to understand and grasp. Making it immaterial helped a little, but not much.

Something important though is that consciousness can be altered based on the alteration of our physical brain - it could be argued that with immaterial minds when your brain is half blown apart your mind shouldn't change, you should just suddenly feel like your body is failing you. I mean I guess we don't know what people actually feel maybe that is the case
