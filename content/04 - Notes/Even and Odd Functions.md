# Even and Odd Functions
---

An even function is such that $f(x)=f(-x)$
An odd function is such that $-f(x)=f(-x)$

A cool feature is that any function can be written as a sum of an even function and an odd function, given by the following proof
$$
\begin{align}
&\frac{1}{2}(h(x)+h(-x)) \\
+&\frac{1}{2}(h(x)-h(-x)) \\
\hline
=&h(x)
\end{align}
$$

This idea is related to symmetrical and skew-symmetrical matrices in [Linear Algebra](/notes/linear-algebra)
