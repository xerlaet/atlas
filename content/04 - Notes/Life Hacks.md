## General Tips
- start the day right
	- wake up at the same time
	- clean yourself
	- eat a high protein breakfast
	- meditate (headspace app?)
- consistency and dedication adds up (motivation is fleeting)
	- utilize pockets of time for something worthwhile
	- eliminate activities you don't want to work 10,000 hours for
- take care of your body
	- exercise
	- eat well
	- take breaks - stretch, go outside
- take care of your mind
	- compare yourself to who you were yesterday, not someone else
	- don't worry about others judging you
- take risks, but be mindful
	- be ok going out of your comfort zone
- consume knowledge from everywhere
	- fiction and non fiction
	- remember that strangers have a complex life as well
- prioritize
- 2 minute rule
- keep an orderly space
- block schedule certain things (checking email, hw, ...)
- Flow state - create a leadup to it?
- stay positive - it will be harder to cave in to your desires
- lucid dreaming?
