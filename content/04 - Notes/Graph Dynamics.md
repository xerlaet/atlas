# Graph Dynamics
---

Normally clustered graphs have a high degree of separation
Random graphs have very low degrees of separation
Taking a clustered graph and adding a small percentage as "shortcuts", results in still high clustering and also low separation! It's a small world

This is where the 6 degrees of separation comes from


In the growth process of a network, if new nodes are more likely to appear to nodes that already have a lot of connections, hubs naturally emerge which is like the Internet, airport routes (O'Hare), important species in a food chain

In the real world, you can change who you hang out with and that ability greatly changes the dynamic - the network is no longer as susceptible to a slight majority
