# Flow
---

the state of being at optimal conditions, everything just works

Wall staring can make work more stimulating, if your break is more stimulating then your brain wants to stay there. If you starve it during a break, you brain will want to come back to work. It could also be something like getting a glass of water, or taking a walk.

Being able to recover from a distraction is very good

If you change your environment, then you feel like you haven't exerted as much effort

You wake up near flow, and you have less ideas going on in your head

To enter flow, take on a bit more than you think you can, then reduce it
	commit to the output, then you will adapt to it
