# Principle of Credulity
---

**Principle of Credulity**: If it seems to you that something is the case, you are justified in believing it is the case unless you have a strong reason to doubt it.

Looking around you, it appears that the physical world is objectively real. You can't prove you are in a simulation or dreaming, but the burden of proof should be on the skeptic to prove the world is an illusion.
