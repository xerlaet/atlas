# Selection Problem
---

Select the $i$th smallest element of an array $A$

## Randomized Selection / Quickselect
quicksort but just traverse one side

expected running time is $O(n)$, worst case is $O(n^{2})$ like [Quick Sort](/notes/quick-sort)

## Median of Medians

---
Linear time

-2 for end not being median


total sum of subproblems must be stricly less than n
