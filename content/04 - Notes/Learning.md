# Learning
---

Learning is the meta skill, akin to the wish for more wishes

## Encoding
1. Ask "why" to deepen understanding and uncover gaps in knowledge
2. Simplify and explain concepts in your own words to a five year old

## Retrieval
1. Connect ideas in a map of knowledge, utilize analogies when possible
2. Utilize [Spaced Repetition](/notes/spaced-repetition) to retain information longer. The spaced repetition will vary depending on the information being tested. For simple facts flashcards work great, for more complicated concepts/skills actually testing yourself is important

![Forgetting Curve](/attachments/Forgetting%20Curve)

Make sure to test your recall/skills the way that you want to be able to remember them. E.g. If you practice multiple choice answers, will you encounter multiple choice answers in the real world?
