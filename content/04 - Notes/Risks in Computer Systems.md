# Risks in Computer Systems
---

## Key Terms
- **Computer system:** A complex arrangement of hardware, software, networks, and people working together to perform specific tasks.
- **Error:** A flaw, mistake, or problem within a computer program or system.
- **Failure:** The inability of a computer system to perform its intended function.
- **Risk:** The potential for harm, loss, or negative consequences associated with the use of a computer system.
- **Safety-critical application:** A computer system where a failure could result in injury or death to people.
- **Legacy system:** An older computer system, software, or piece of hardware that is still in use within an organization.
- **Repetitive Strain Injury (RSI):** Physical pain or injury caused by repetitive motions, often associated with using electronic devices or keyboards.
- **High Reliability Organization (HRO):** An organization that operates in environments with high potential for failure but maintains a high level of safety and performance.
- **Preoccupation with failure:** A characteristic of HROs where the organization actively anticipates and prepares for potential errors or unexpected events.
- **Hazard analysis:** Techniques used by system designers to identify potential risks and develop strategies to mitigate them.
- **User interface:** The way a computer system interacts with its human users, including displays, input methods, and feedback.
- **Redundancy:** The inclusion of duplicate components or processes in a system so that if one fails, another can take over.
- **Self-checking:** Software or hardware that verifies its own results or operations to detect errors.
- **Independent Verification and Validation (IV&V):** Testing and validation of software by an organization separate from the development team and the customer.
- **Beta testing:** A stage of software testing where a near-final version of a product is used by a selected group of real-world users to identify bugs and issues.
- **TCAS (Traffic Collision Avoidance System):** A system in aircraft that detects potential mid-air collisions and instructs pilots on avoidance maneuvers.
- **GPWS (Ground Proximity Warning System):** A system in aircraft that warns pilots if the plane is too close to the ground or obstacles like mountains.
- **Criminal negligence:** Behavior that shows a reckless disregard for the safety or well-being of others, potentially leading to criminal charges in cases of system failure.
- **Civil penalties:** Legal consequences or judgments that require a party to pay compensation for damages caused by faulty systems.
- **Regulation:** Rules and requirements established by government agencies to ensure the safety and reliability of products or systems, particularly in safety-critical areas.
