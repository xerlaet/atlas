# The Dentures Man NDE
---

Not many details have been released about this, but here is what we know:

A 44-year-old man was brought into a hospital emergency room in deep cardiac arrest. While he was clinically dead with no pulse or breathing, a nurse removed the patient's dentures to insert a breathing tube and placed them on a sliding wooden shelf of a crash cart.

They performed several rounds of chest compressions, and eventually the patient recovered.

Days layer, after being resuscitated and regaining consciousness, the patient saw the same nurse and told him "you know where my dentures are". He also mentioned how he was conscious during part of it and wanted to tell them they didn't need to press so hard because he felt pain from the chest compressions but couldn't say anything.

## Skeptical Response
The nurse acknowledged that by the time the mechanical chest compression device was operating, circulation might have been sufficient for pain perception, even though the patient didn't show any signs of consciousness.

It's possible that his brain was awake and heard the distinct voice of the nurse, heard his dentures being placed onto the wooden crash card, and briefly saw the nurse checking his pupils.
