# Calculus
---

**Calculus** is the study of continuous change

## Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
$$
\begin{align}
\frac{d}{dx}\int_{a}^x f(x) \, dx &= f(x) \\
\int_{a}^b f'(x) \, dx &= f(b)-f(a)
\end{align}
$$

## Limits and Continuity
The limit of $f(x)$ as $x$ approaches $c$ is written as
$$
\begin{align}
\lim_{ x \to c } f(x)&=L \text{ if and only if} \\
\lim_{ x \to c^- } f(x)&=\lim_{ x \to c^+ } f(x)=L
\end{align}
$$
A function is continuous at $c$ when the following is met
$$
\begin{align}
&f(c)\text{ is defined} \\
&\lim_{ x \to c } f(x) \text{ exists} \\
&\lim_{ x \to c } f(x) =f(c)
\end{align}
$$

## Sequences and Series
[Sequences](/notes/sequences)

A **sequence** is a list of numbers in a specific order $\{ a_{n} \}$

If $\lim_{ n \to \infty } a_{n}$ exists then the sequence is **convergent**, otherwise it is **divergent** (to $\infty$ or $-\infty$)

Properties of sequences

A **series** is the sum of all the terms in a sequence $\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_{n}$, which results as just a single number (assuming it makes sense to compute the sequence)

Properties of series

Convergence and Divergence

Taylor and Maclaurin series
