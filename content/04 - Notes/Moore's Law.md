# Moore's Law
---

**Moore's Law** is the observation that the number of transistors in integrated circuits doubles every two years and has held up surprisingly well historically

![[Pasted image 20250123132658.png]]

Eventually it will hit a limit due to the laws of physics. Currently transistors are running into atomic barriers, which might be able to be overcome with sub atomic transistors, but will still likely hit a limit somewhere. Also the speed of light and other constants of nature play a role.

Moore's second law, also known as Rock's law (after Arthur Rock), is that the cost of a microfabrication plant also increases exponentially over time
