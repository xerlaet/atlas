# Analyzing Everything
---

The first time I can remember thinking about this is when I was watching a video from Tesla about how they engineer their cars (video link!) and their design process. They analyze every part of the car and determine if it should be there or not. It could be something super common in every other car, but they will still analyze it because sometimes the majority is wrong or doing something suboptimal. In doing so, they know that every part of their car is there for a reason and contributes to their overall goal.

This can apply to someone's life as well. I have been spending some time analyzing everything that I have in my life and whether or not it should be there. Take Instagram for example, I have not been using it at all and I don't really intend on picking it up anytime soon either. I don't see any real benefit except the occasional connection with someone else, but that doesn't seem worth it. So I deleted my account (which was really hard by the way, Meta doesn't want you to) and now my phone has one less app to clutter up my screen. And it's another account that I don't have to worry about upkeeping and spending time on in my day, since let's be honest, there are plenty of better things to spend my time on then scrolling through shorts about various random topics that are engaging.

Sometimes Engineers have some great ideas.
