# Near Death Experiences
---

**Near Death Experiences** are where individuals report out-of-body observations where they may have learned specific, verifiable facts about their physical surroundings or future events where they shouldn't have been able to know them.

## Analysis
Apparently anesthesia awareness occurs for 1 or 2 out of every 1,000 procedures. It's not out of the question that NDEs are simply those cases mixed with some fragments of memories patched together by the brain as a cohesive out-of-body experience.

Additionally, there have been controlled clinical tests on cardiac arrest patients:
- AWARE I (2014) - Researchers placed hidden images on shelves in rooms where cardiac arrests were likely to occur. Out of 2,060 cardiac arrest patients, 330 survived and 140 were well enough to complete interviews. 9 experienced classical NDEs, and 2 reported specific visual awareness of their physical resuscitation from an out-of-body perspective. However, none of the patients saw the specific hidden images that were placed in shelves. One patient was able to describe the physical actions of doctors and the sounds of the defibrillator, but he wasn't in a room where the shelves were installed.
- AWARE II (2023) - Researchers attended CPR events with EEG devices and an iPad face-up above the patient, displaying a rotating set of images while playing audio words through headphones. The EEG detected spikes of brain activity associated with higher cognitive function up to 60 minutes into CPR. 28 survivors were interviewed, several reporting lucid experiences or hearing medical conversations. One recalled the specific audio words played, however zero successfully identified the visual images.

## Stance
Honestly all of these NDEs seem to have plausible naturalistic explanations. While I know there are many more, from my knowledge these are five of the best out there. If this is the best that NDEs have to offer, I'm not that convinced.
