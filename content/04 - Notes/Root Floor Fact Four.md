# Root Floor Fact Four
---

Conjectured by [[Donald Knuth]], the idea is that every positive integer can be created with a single 4 and some combination of square roots, floors, and factorials

$$
\left\lfloor \sqrt{\sqrt{\sqrt{\sqrt{\sqrt{(4!)!}}}}} \right\rfloor =5
$$

This problem has an infinite state space due to the factorial always yielding a larger integer
