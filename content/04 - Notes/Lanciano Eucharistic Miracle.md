# Lanciano Eucharistic Miracle
---
![Pasted image 20260927161619.png](/attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260927161619.png)
According to tradition, a monk was doubting the true presence of Christ when during mass the Eucharist changed into flesh and blood.

The blood coagulated into five distinct globules, and both have been preserved and remain on display to this day. The center has a gaping hole, and pin marks were observed around the outside, implying it was pinned to a board at one time.

In 1970, Dr. Odoardo Linoli did an examination on them. He noted:
1. The flesh is human myocardium from the left ventricle
2. The flesh and blood are of human origin
3. The blood type is AB
4. The proteins in the blood showed the same percentage as found in fresh human blood
5. No trace of chemical preservatives or embalming substances were found

The actual microscope photographs were published in a secular, peer-reviewed, Italian medical journal, and the consensus is that the slides legitimately show human heart tissue. Any debate largely has to focus on the origins of the tissue and the methods used to test it.

## Analysis
**Historical Gap**: The earliest documented mentions don't appear until the 16th century (an 800 year gap).

**Carbon-14**: Is it possible it is a mummified piece of a human heart taken from a medieval cadaver? No Carbon-14 test has been allowed. The Church's rationale is the required destruction of part of the relic and the likelihood of inconclusive findings due to centuries of heavy contamination. See the [Shroud of Turin](/notes/shroud-of-turin).

**Anatomical Knowledge**: A theist would posit that a medieval forger would not know how to isolate a left ventricle, endocardium, and vagus nerve, cleanly slicing it into a wafer-thin, circular cross-section without destroying the tissue.

**Methodology**: Dr. Linoli's investigation was essentially done alone. His co-investigator, Dr. Ruggero Bertelli, wasn't even there for the tissue sampling or preparation of the slides.

**Contamination**: Dr. Linoli's report noted that the relics were heavily contaminated with fungus and spores, a test for AB antigens could easily have yielded a false positive.

**Preservation**: Supposedly human tissue can naturally mummify and endure for centuries on its own without chemical embalming, particularly when sealed inside a glass reliquary.

**The WHO Fraud**: It was touted that in 1973 the WHO appointed a UN-backed study with over 500 tests over 15 months, validating Linoli's findings. However, Dr. Franco Serafini tracked down the WHO report which was kept inside the Lanciano monastery. He found someone has printed a fake front and back cover and wrapped it around hundreds of pages of an unrelated scientific study about Egyptian mummies. Dr. Linoli, who has cited this study, could very well be a victim of this fraud.

## Skeptical Theory
By the 1500's which was when the host first entered the historical record, advanced cardiac dissections were taking place in Renaissance Italy. A skilled anatomist or forger sliced a piece of a cadaver's heart and pinned it to a board to dry in the shape of a host (which accounts for the pinholes and torn center). At that point, rapid desiccation meant there was no moisture left for bacteria to fully decompose it. However, bacteria and fungi from the environment would still accumulate on the surface over the centuries.

When Dr. Linoli tested the blood, he had to rehydrate (dissolve) the dried clots to use electrophoresis. The test proved that the proportion of proteins was consistent with fresh human blood. This is biologically expected, as proteins in a stable, dry environment will decay at a proportional rate. Finally, the AB blood reading was simply a misfire caused by the heavy bacterial and fungal presence.
