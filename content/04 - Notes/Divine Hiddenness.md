# Divine Hiddenness
---

1. If God exists, He would desire a personal, loving relationship with all of His creatures
2. Non-resistant non-believers exist
3. Therefore God does not exist

## Analysis
Are there any non-resistant non-believers? Perhaps everyone who doesn't believe has hidden internal biases blocking them from doing so.

Perhaps there is a reason that God would allow for someone to not believe in Him. If His existence was blindingly obvious, we might be coerced out of terror or self-preservation to believe in Him. Counter example: fallen angels.
