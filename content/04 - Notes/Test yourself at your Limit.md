# Test yourself at your Limit
---

Growth happens when you push yourself. Push yourself too hard and you will fail. Don't push yourself hard enough and you will plateau. There is a range where if you push yourself at that level you will succeed with effort, and grow because of it.

This applies to working out (progressive overload), remembering information ([Spaced Repetition](/notes/spaced-repetition)), and life overall.

Straddle the edge with one foot in [Order](/notes/order) and the other in [Chaos](/notes/chaos).
