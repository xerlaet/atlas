## 1-2: Failures with the Canaanites
**Incomplete Conquest**
- Israel fails to fully drive out the Canaanites (Judg 1).
- Disobedience leads to coexistence with pagan nations.

**Cycle Begins**
- Israel turns to idolatry.
- God allows oppression as consequence (Judg 2).
- Pattern established: sin → oppression → repentance → deliverance.

## 3-16: Corruption of the Judges
**Cycle of Sin**
- Israel repeatedly falls into idolatry.
- God raises judges to deliver them.
- Peace is temporary; cycle repeats.

**The Judges**
- Othniel, Ehud, Deborah, Gideon, Jephthah, Samson.
- Each judge shows increasing moral weakness.

**Downward Spiral**
- From faithful leaders to deeply flawed ones.
- Samson represents strength corrupted by sin.

## 17-21: Corruption of the People
**Moral Chaos**
- Idolatry spreads throughout Israel (Judg 17-18).
- Religious corruption and false worship.

**Social Breakdown**
- Extreme violence and injustice (Judg 19-21).
- Israel resembles the nations around them.

**No King in Israel**
- “Everyone did what was right in his own eyes” (Judg 21:25).
- Absence of authority leads to disorder.

## Core Theological Takeaways
- Sin leads to a **cycle of destruction** without true repentance.
- Partial obedience is still **disobedience**.
- God is merciful, repeatedly raising deliverers.
- Human leaders are insufficient → need for a **true righteous king**.
- Points forward to Christ as the definitive deliverer.
