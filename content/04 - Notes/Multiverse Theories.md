# Multiverse Theories
---

Multiverse generating models often require fine tuning of their own so they just push the problem back a step. Here are some better formulations of a multiverse:
- everything possible exists
- every consistent mathematical structure describes a universe
- every logically consistent universe exists
- Steven Wolfram's [Theory of Everything](https://writings.stephenwolfram.com/2020/04/finally-we-may-have-a-path-to-the-fundamental-theory-of-physics-and-its-beautiful/)

Steven Wolfram's has a lot of critiques, mostly that it can't really prove anything.

Two larger problems for these philosophical multiverses are:
1. We should expect to be Boltzmann brains
2. We should expect the universe to devolve into chaos at every moment

Can our experience provide a strong enough prior to overcome the likelihood of not being a Boltzmann brain?

Expecting the universe to devolve into chaos is a crazy belief to have. Perhaps is there a way to show that universes with simpler laws are more likely? Perhaps with [Kolmogorov Complexity](/notes/kolmogorov-complexity)?
