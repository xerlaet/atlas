# Pomodoro Method
---

The **Pomodoro Method** is a technique for time management. Work is allotted 25 minute intervals with 5 minute intervals in between. Every four cycles you take a 15 minute break instead of a 5 minute one.

Short break ideas:
- drink some water or eat a snack
- walk around inside
- listen to music
- stretch
- breathe
- tidy your workspace
- check emails or messages
- switch study spots
- journal
- work on a todo list

Long break ideas:
- go for a walk
- eat a prepared meal
- socialize
- read a book
- power nap
- play piano
- guided meditation
