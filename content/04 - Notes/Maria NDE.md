# Maria NDE
---

In 1977, a migrant worker named Maria suffered severe cardiac arrest at Harborview Medical Center in Seattle. She was resuscitated and later spoke with the critical care social worker Kimberly Clark Sharp. She claimed to have floated out of her body, out the hospital doors, and up the side of the building and saw a dark blue tennis shoe on the third-floor window ledge. She noted that the little toe was worn through and one of the shoelaces was tucked under the heel.

## Skeptical Response
In 1996 three researchers visited the hospital and placed a tennis shoe on the specific 3rd floor ledge. They saw that it was clearly visible from the ground outside the hospital, so it's possible that Maria was able to see the shoe as she was wheeled into the building.

The researchers also found that the shoe on the specific ledge would have been easily visible from inside the hospital room so Maria could have seen it from her bed, unlike what the social worker said where she had to press her face flat against the glass to even see it. It's also possible that someone else saw it, mentioned it in Maria's room, and Maria heard it and formed it into a memory.

Ultimately, Maria was never able to be tracked down to be re-interviewed, and no one besides the social worker ever verified the shoe's existence in the first place.
