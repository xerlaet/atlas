# Problem of Petitionary Prayer
---

God is immutable, yet why then do we pray to Him asking for things to be different or a certain way?

## Analysis
Perhaps praying is the means by which God achieves His purposes. His mind doesn't change, but we do as an effect of prayer. Perhaps He sets things up so that if we pray in a certain way/amount, then something will come about as a result of that, and it is fully conditional upon us.
