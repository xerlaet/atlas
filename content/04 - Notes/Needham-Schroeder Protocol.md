# Needham-Schroeder Protocol
---

A **trusted intermediary protocol** that uses a KDC to distribute a session key and authenticate two parties through challenge-response

![TikZ Diagram](/attachments/tikz-79cd7a4a1aa87f2c5f568cabfd77d3c2.svg)

The **Expanded Needham-Schroeder Protocol** gives Bob freshness guarantees by including his nonce in the KDC-issued ticket

![TikZ Diagram](/attachments/tikz-a82a1463e4e9a7eedd41e3cbcd8e96bf.svg)

## Security
- **Nonce $N_A$**: Lets Alice verify that the KDC's response matches her request
- **Session key $K_{AB}$**: Generated by the KDC and shared between Alice and Bob
- **Mutual authentication**: Nonce challenge-response lets both parties demonstrate knowledge of $K_{AB}$
- **Limitation**: Bob cannot verify that a ticket containing an old session key is fresh, leaving the protocol vulnerable to replay attacks
