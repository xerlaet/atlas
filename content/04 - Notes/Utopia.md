# Utopia
---

In a scenario where everything that can be automated is, what is left to do? Is there purpose or meaning in life besides the obvious answer of religion?

Every activity that humans could do could be done better by robots. Any sport, any task, any discussion. Robots would be better at everything. Super drugs would exist that could give you immense happiness and pleasure at a moment's notice with zero side effects and be able to last indefinitely with prolonged close to infinite human lifespans.

What is left? Is life worthwhile at all?

You could artificially put yourself in a specific situation that would test your skills and give you a purpose in order to not die or to achieve something that you set up for, similar to a rock climber halfway up a cliff face.

Of course that might be some people that simply prefer human created objects or human run businesses simply because they aren't robots even if the product/service is inferior.



There might not be something that makes life truly worthwhile, or some sort of actual objective purpose. For that you might need God. On the contrary, we don't know very much. We know (with reasonable confidence) that we have this one life to live, and in it we can do anything within the human experience. We don't know if there is anything outside (God, simulation, infinite worlds, rebirth), so it might make sense to at the very least just make the most of what we have even if it might end up being pointless, or the opposite that it is extremely important.
