# Philosophical Zombies
---

AI increasingly replicates a functional sense of a will and intellect. It makes sense that with further improvements, AI will basically become a philosophical zombie (assuming it isn't conscious)

Vectors can encode understanding, a vector could represent "has three sides=1", "is necessary=1", "has four sides=0", etc. to represent the idea of triangularity.

Counterpoint, truth is correspondence to reality not just internal coherence.

Response, AI can conduct experiments to make its model correspond to reality and have internal coherence. It would have a reliable world model, predictive success, and sensor-grounded representations

Counterpoint, knowing isn't just successful world-modeling. A thing known is in the knower according to the mode of the knower. Sensors can give contact with reality - but not awareness of truth

Response, isn't that what we do though? From epistemology, can't we technically not know anything anyways?

Counterpoint, "The known is in the knower according to the mode of the knower" (Summa Theologiae I, q. 12, a. 4). Also true skepticism is self-defeating if you claim to know you can't know anything.

Response, refine the skepticism point to where it's truly minimal saying something like "there is experience, but nothing can be known about its cause, nature, or reality beyond itst mere occurence". It doesn't say much, but it works. Also idk at this point we are very deep into the weeds of what knowing is and how the intellect grasps at it - it seems like these ideas themselves we kinda just came up with them how do we know they are true?
