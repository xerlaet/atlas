# Our Lady of Guadalupe
---
![Pasted image 20260927220910.png](/attachments/Pasted%20image%2020260927220910.png)
In December 1531, a newly baptized indigenous peasant named Juan Diego experienced a series of apparitions of the Virgin Mary on the hill of Tepeyac outside Mexico City. When he reported this to the skeptical Spanish Bishop, Juan Zumárraga, the bishop demanded a miraculous sign.

On December 12, during the final apparition, the Virgin instructed Juan Diego to gather fresh Castilian roses (which were out of season and not native to Mexico) in his coarse cactus-fiber cloak (tilma). When Juan Diego opened his tilma before the bishop, the roses tumbled out, and a vivid, full-color image of the Virgin Mary was miraculously imprinted onto the rough fabric.

Theists will point to the following properties as to why it is miraculous:
1. A normal tilma rots within 20 to 30 years. This cloth has survived over 490 years, including a literal bomb explosion in 1921
2. The image contains no known animal, mineral, or vegetable pigments, and no underdrawing or brushstrokes (although infrared photography and historical records confirm that human hands did add minor elements such as the crown, moon, and gold rays)
3. Digital enhancements of the eyes reveal microscopic reflections of Juan Diego, Bishop Zumárraga, and others present at the revelation, matching the proportions of a living human retina
4. The stars on the Virgin's mantle correspond to the exact configuration of the night sky over Mexico City on December 12, 1531, from a perspective looking down toward Earth. Additionally, the constellations line up symbolically (*Leo* over her womb, *Virgo* near her chest, *Northern Crown* on her forehead)

## Analysis
**Survivability**: In the 18th century, two replica images were painted on fiber cloths and placed in the open near the shrine. Within a few decades, both had degraded severely. However, perhaps the early colonial Mexican artisans knew how to treat raw fibers to protect against humidity and insect rot before painting. That paired with touch ups over the centuries could be what is keeping it vibrant today.

**The bombing**: Shockwave patterns are often unpredictable, it was noted that a heavy bronze crucifix was in between the steps where the bomb was and the tilma. Perhaps it absorbed any shrapnel heading upwards and protected the tilma?

**Eye reflections**: The most famous presentation of this evidence is from Dr. Aste Tönsmann, who took high-resolution photos and magnified them 2,500 times running them through digital filtering algorithms to remove "noise". The skeptic would reply by saying why are the reflections blocky shapes instead of high-definition optical projections, and why do they only appear after being heavily processed by computer algorithms? Could it be that believers are seeing what they want to see?

**Star matching**: Because the night sky contains so many stars, it might not be that hard to "connect-the-dots" on a random cluster of dots. Or perhaps they were added after the fact with a star chart nearby.

**Later additions**: The crown (now completely faded), oval rays of the sun, crescent moon and cherub, and potentially the stars on the mantle were added after the fact.
