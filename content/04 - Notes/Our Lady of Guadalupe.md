# Our Lady of Guadalupe
---

San Juan Diego was a native who was one of the first to be baptized by the Franciscan missionaries. In the morning on December 9, 1535, he saw the first apparition telling him to build a temple on the hill. He went to the bishop but the bishop was skeptical. Juan Diego returned that night and the Virgin reaffirmed his mission. The bishop asked for a sign, and on December 10th a third apparition told him to return the next morning. Juan Diego's uncle was gravely ill, so instead of going to the hill he went to look for a priest but saw the apparition for a fourth time, who told him to climb the hill and cut roses for the bishop. He went to the bishop and as he unfolded the tilma which was carrying the roses, the famous image appeared.

Additions and touch ups were added, however the original parts of the image and its preservation is largely unexplained

Making a painting without brushstrokes seems to be very possible, just difficult

Believers point to the survivability of the painting as a sign of its miraculous properties - both from a bomb and just over time

Believers will also point to the stars aligning, microscopic people in the eyes, and music from the tilma. These don't seem that convincing though
