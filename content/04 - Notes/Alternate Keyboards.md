The corpus used really matters.
**Bigrams**: Two letter sequences
**Trigrams**: Three letter sequences

**Same Finger bigrams** (SFBs): Pressing two keys in succession with the same finger. Generally these are really avoided, but if they exist ideally they are on the index and middle fingers

**Same finger skipgrams** (SFSs): Pressing two keys with the same finger, but separated by X letters. Ideally low distance and many keys of separation

**Full scissor bigrams** (FSBs): the vertical separation between two keys is two rows, and the finger that prefers being higher is not

**Lateral stretch bigrams** (LSBs): bigram that pulls two fingers apart or forces the wrist to shift laterally


**Alternate**: pressing one key with one hand, then one with the other, and back (e.g. AND)
**Roll**: pressing two keys with one hand, and a third with the other (e.g. OUR)
**3Roll**: one-handed trigram where all keys go in the same direction (e.g. WER)
**Redirect**: one-handed trigram in which the direction changes (eg. SAD)
