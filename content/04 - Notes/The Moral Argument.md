# The Moral Argument
---

1. Objective moral values only exist if God exists
2. Objective moral values do exist
3. Therefore God exists

Something else to consider, is that we have an intuitive understanding of reality and we all accept that there is an objective reality. Why would a similar line of reasoning not work for objective morality?

## Response
Perhaps objective moral values don't exist. Something to distinguish between the lines of reasoning for reality vs morality is that there is much more agreement about reality. For morality, there is a lot of discussion about LGBTQ+-!#9... whatever it is, along with different cultural traditions throughout time where killing others in revenge was just or right. Whereas that same level of disagreement hasn't really been seen about reality (although I guess the existence of God might be an argument for that lol)
