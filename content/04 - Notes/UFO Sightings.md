# UFO Sightings
---

[National UFO Reporting Center](https://nuforc.org)

Since their founding in 1974, they have processed over 180,000 reports of UFO sightings.

Does that mean aliens exist? Probably not. It's almost certain that a large majority can be explained away by fake reports, various human controlled aerial vehicles, or atmospheric illusions. Odds are the rest can also be explained away without involving aliens or any higher powers. But it's interesting to study and think about, especially when considering how to evaluate evidence of miraculous apparitions from various religious groups.
