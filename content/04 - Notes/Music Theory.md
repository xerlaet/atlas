# Music Theory
---

The standard treble and bass clef with their respective notes:
```music-abc
T:
M:
L:1
K:C
[V:T clef=treble] _ _  "C"C"D"D"E"E"F"F"G"G"A"A"B"B"C"c"D"d"E"e"F"f"G"g"A"a|
[V:B clef=bass] "E"E,, "F"F,, "G"G,, "A"A,, "B"B,, "C"C, "D"D, "E"E, "F"F, "G"G, "A"A, "B"B, "C"C|
```

An **octave** is the interval between and including two notes where one is twice or half the frequency of vibration of the other

A **semitone** is a half step whereas a **tone** is a full step

Every scale follows the pattern of two tones, a semitone, three tones, and a final semitone. The **tonic** is the note on which a note starts from

There are seven different modes which are based on the idea of starting at a different note and playing all of the white notes on the piano until the full scale has been reached

C (Ionian)
D (Dorian)
E (Phrygian)
F (Lydian)
G (Mixolydian)
A (Aeolian)
B (Locrian)
