# Sharing Ideas
---
When someone has a really good idea or concept about something, they will want to share it with others. This will repeat recursively until more and more people know about it. So if something is very niche or not well understood, likely it is not a good or useful idea or it hasn't been phrased as such yet.

This makes me think about grand ideas of the universe, if there is such an idea it would seem to be extremely valuable and insightful and something that one would share with others until the majority of humans know about it. So the fact that there isn't such a thing makes me believe that such an idea hasn't been come across yet or that it is still being created with the advancements that we have today.
