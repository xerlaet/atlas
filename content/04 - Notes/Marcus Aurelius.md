# Marcus Aurelius
---

Roman Emperor from 161 to 180, the last emperor during Pax Romana which was an age of peace, calm, and stability for the empire from 27 BCE to 180 CE

His untitled private notes later known as Meditations is one of the best sources for the Stoicism philosophy

**Stoicism** teaches that resilience, self-control, and virtue is the path to a good life. We should acknowledge that some things are out of our control, be mindful of death as a natural part of life, and maintaining inner peace
