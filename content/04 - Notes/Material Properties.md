# Material Properties
---

**Material Properties** are the intensive physical and chemical characteristics of a substance that determine its behavior and response to external stimuli like forces or temperature changes. These properties are independent of the amount of the material.

## States of Matter
The primary physical states a substance can exist in, determined by temperature and pressure.
- **Solid**: Definite shape and volume; particles are in fixed positions.
- **Liquid**: Indefinite shape but definite volume; particles can flow past one another.
- **Gas**: Indefinite shape and volume; particles move randomly and fill their container.
- **Plasma**: An ionized gas consisting of ions and electrons, the most abundant state in the universe.

## Mechanical & Thermal Properties
- **Density ($\rho$)**: Mass per unit volume.
- **Elasticity**: A material's ability to deform under stress and return to its original shape when the stress is removed. This is detailed further in [Stress and Strain](/notes/stress-and-strain).
- **Thermal Conductivity**: The ability of a material to transfer heat.
- **Specific Heat Capacity**: The amount of heat required to raise the temperature of a unit mass of a substance by one degree.
