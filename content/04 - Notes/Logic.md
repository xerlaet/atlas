# Logic
---

**Logic** is the study of reasoning and inference, providing a framework for evaluating the soundness of arguments

[Modes of Reasoning](/notes/modes-of-reasoning)

An argument is **valid** if the conclusion follows from the premises, and **sound** if additionally the premises are actually true
