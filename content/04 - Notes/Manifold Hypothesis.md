# Manifold Hypothesis
---

![TikZ Diagram](/attachments/tikz-44db3e3ef8c63879bccd67aa40f15b8b.svg)

Although data may have many features (**ambient dimension**), its underlying structure (**intrinsic dimension**) often has far fewer degrees of freedom

For example, images contain thousands of pixel values, but meaningful variations may be governed by fewer factors, such as pose, lighting, and object identity
