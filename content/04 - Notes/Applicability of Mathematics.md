# Applicability of Mathematics
---

The "unreasonable effectiveness of mathematics" is very surprising under naturalism. Why in the world would the universe operate under these nice mathematical principles?

## Analysis
Mathematics is more like a descriptive language we invented to model reality. If reality was different, math would be different to match it. With quantum mechanics we invented some new math to help understand it. When we observe things, we come up with the simplest equations we can to match it.

What about mathematical predictions like Paul Dirac predicting anti-matter before it was discovered, or Einstein using Riemann's non-Euclidean geometry for General Relativity?

I would think that our human brains were developed to understand however the universe functioned. And if we couldn't come about in the first place, then that is really the fine-tuning argument instead of an argument from mathematics

Also there are many things like fluids or the weather that is chaotic and non-linear where it practically impossible to model it with a mathematical model and so the best we can do is estimate or use machine learning to guess and macro patterns

## Stance
While it's a neat argument, I think it just falls completely flat. Whenever we want to understand something we develop math around it. And if we couldn't understand something, what would that even look like? Perhaps consciousness but if that is the case then math is no longer applicable so the argument doesn't even work.
