# Interior Design
---

## Lighting
Have multiple light sources (4-9) to add depth

General lighting (overhead) - get a dimmer!
Task lighting - lighting for specific places
Accent lighting - add personality

Use warmer lighting (~2700k)

## Work in 3 Dimensions
Decorate the walls!

Add curtains (rod 6" above what it's covering, extend 12" horizontally each direction, and should just barely kiss the floor). They should also be double the width of what they are covering so they will always have ripples

The focal point of artwork should be 57" off the floor. Art behind a couch or bed should be no longer than 2/3 the width. Also always frame artwork

## Textures Before Colors
Prioritize organics (plants, wood) before in-organics (metal, glass) and finally synthetic (plastic, acrylic)

## Furnish Actions
Think about how the room can function better. This will often look nice as a side effect (ex. where should a lamp go? by the chair to read in)

Think about the command position. Don't have it facing away from the door, or facing directly toward the door. Also consider traffic, make sure conversation areas of a room are within 10ft in diameter

## Practical, Beautiful, Personal
Make sure everything on display is one of these. Make sure they tell something about you

## Clean it up
Make sure the room is tidy (80% of items should be stored out of site)
