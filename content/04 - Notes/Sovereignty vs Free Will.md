# Sovereignty vs Free Will
---

How can we choose our actions freely and God be the ultimate cause of everything at the same time? It would imply that there are actions which break the causal chain (free will)

But even if free will exists, doesn't our environment play a large role in shaping who we are? And at least for the first humans (Adam and Eve), that environment was directly set up by God


## Response
While the environment set up by God does impact us a lot, perhaps there's part of our souls that are still independent (non deterministic and non random)
