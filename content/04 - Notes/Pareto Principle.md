# Pareto Principle
---

The realization that for many outcomes, 80 percent of the results come from 20 percent of the input

The fact is that with 20 percent of the effort, 80 percent of the results can often be achieved

 This can be applied recursively, such that the 20 percent of each 20 percent can be focused on first for faster and larger gains (4% for 64% on two levels)
