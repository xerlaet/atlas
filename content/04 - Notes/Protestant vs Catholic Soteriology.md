# Protestant vs Catholic Soteriology
---

**Soteriology** (the study of salvation) differs between [Protestantism](/notes/protestantism) and [Catholicism](/notes/catholicism) primarily in how God applies righteousness. Is righteousness an external legal declaration ([Imputed Righteousness](/notes/imputed-righteousness)), or an internal transformation through grace ([Infused Righteousness](/notes/infused-righteousness))?

![TikZ Diagram](/attachments/tikz-feef009939f96b9a4422d6c2e6ad9bbe.svg)

| Concept                  | Protestantism                                                               | Catholicism                                                                    |
| :----------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Mechanism**            | Credited externally via legal declaration                                   | Poured internally to transform the soul                                        |
| **Justification**        | Complete instantly via faith alone ([Sola Fide](/notes/sola-fide))                          | Ongoing process via faith, grace, and works                                    |
| **Sanctification**       | A distinct, secondary process following justification                       | Merged into the same ongoing process as justification                          |
| **Faith w/o Works**      | **DNE** (A lack of works proves faith is dead/false)                        | **Not Saved** (True faith can exist without works, but it loses justification) |
| **Post-Death Mechanism** | **Glorification**: God instantly and sovereignly removes all sin upon death | **Purgatory**: Cleansing fire purifies a saved soul of residual venial sin     |
| **Scriptural Basis**     | [Romans 4:5](/notes/romans-45), [2 Cor 5:21](/notes/2-cor-521), [Ephesians 2:8-9](/notes/ephesians-28-9)                         | [James 2:24](/notes/james-224), [2 Peter 1:4](/notes/2-peter-14), [Philippians 2:12-13](/notes/philippians-212-13)                       |
