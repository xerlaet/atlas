## 1-5: Joshua Leads Israel
**Joshua as New Moses**
- Joshua succeeds Moses and leads Israel (Josh 1).
- Called to be strong and faithful to the Law.

**Entering the Promised Land**
- Spies sent into the land; Rahab helps them (Josh 2).
- The Jordan River parts → Israel crosses into the land (Josh 3–4).
- Parallels the Red Sea; fulfillment of God’s promise begins.

**Covenant Renewal**
- Circumcision and Passover observed (Josh 5).
- Israel recommits to the covenant before conquest.

## 6-12: Battles with the Canaanites
**Jericho**
- Walls fall through obedience to God’s command (Josh 6).
- Victory comes from faith, not military strength.

**Ai**
- Israel defeated due to Achan’s hidden sin (Josh 7).
- Sin affects the whole community.
- After repentance, Israel is victorious (Josh 8).

**Conquest of the Land**
- Victories over Canaanite kings (Josh 10–12).
- God fights for Israel and fulfills His promises.

## 13–-22: Joshua Divides the Land
**Inheritance**
- Land distributed among the tribes (Josh 13–21).
- Fulfillment of God’s promise to Abraham (Gen 12:7).

**Unity of Israel**
- Eastern tribes return home after aiding conquest (Josh 22).
- Potential division resolved → unity preserved.

## 23-24: Joshua’s Final Words
**Call to Faithfulness**
- Joshua urges Israel to remain faithful to the covenant (Josh 23).
- Warning against idolatry.

**Covenant Renewal**
- “Choose this day whom you will serve… as for me and my house, we will serve the Lord” (Josh 24:15).
- Israel renews commitment to God.

**Joshua’s Death**
- Joshua dies after leading Israel into the Promised Land.
- Transition to the next stage of Israel’s history.

## Core Theological Takeaways
- God is **faithful to His promises**.
- Victory comes through **obedience and trust**, not human strength.
- Sin has **communal consequences**.
- God fulfills His covenant across generations.
- The Promised Land points to a **greater eternal inheritance** (CCC 1222).
