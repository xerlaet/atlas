# Championship Mentality
---

The **Championship Mentality** is recognizing the long term goal, and being ok with losses in the short term in order to prepare and achieve the long term goal.

For example, learning how to study will take away from time spent working on other things, but in the long term it will be one of the most important skills to have to catapult myself to where I want to be.

Life is the championship, there's still many games to be played.

An important point is that time is not the differentiator here that makes the short term losses worth it. Actions are what matter.
