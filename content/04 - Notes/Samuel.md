## 1-7: Samuel and the Fall of Eli’s House
**Samuel’s Birth and Calling**
- Hannah prays for a son; Samuel is dedicated to God (1 Sam 1).
- God calls Samuel as a prophet (1 Sam 3).

**Corruption of Priests**
- Eli’s sons are corrupt and abuse their role.
- God judges Eli’s house.

**Ark of the Covenant**
- Israel treats the Ark as a tool → defeated by Philistines (1 Sam 4).
- God shows His power even in foreign lands (1 Sam 5–6).

## 8-15: Israel Demands a King
**Request for a King**
- Israel asks for a king “like other nations” (1 Sam 8).
- Rejection of God as their true king.

**Saul as King**
- Saul is chosen and anointed (1 Sam 10).
- Early success followed by disobedience.

**Saul’s سقوط**
- Saul rejects God’s command (1 Sam 15).
- God rejects Saul as king.

## 16-31: Rise of David
**David Anointed**
- David chosen by God, a man after His own heart (1 Sam 16).
- God looks at the heart, not outward appearance.

**David and Goliath**
- David defeats Goliath through faith (1 Sam 17).
- Victory comes from trust in God.

**Saul and David**
- Saul grows jealous and pursues David.
- David refuses to harm Saul, trusting God’s timing.

**Saul’s Death**
- Saul dies in battle (1 Sam 31).

## 1-10: David’s Kingdom Established
**David Becomes King**
- David unites Israel and establishes Jerusalem as capital (2 Sam 5).

**Ark Brought to Jerusalem**
- God’s presence brought into the city (2 Sam 6).

**Davidic Covenant**
- God promises David an eternal kingdom (2 Sam 7).
- Fulfilled in Christ, the eternal King (CCC 709).

## 11-20: David’s Sin and Its Consequences
**David and Bathsheba**
- David commits adultery and murder (2 Sam 11).
- Confronted by Nathan → repents (2 Sam 12).

**Consequences**
- Disorder enters David’s family.
- Violence and rebellion (Absalom).

## 21-24: Final Years of David
**Justice and Judgment**
- Famine and consequences tied to past sin.

**David’s Repentance**
- David sins in taking a census (2 Sam 24).
- Repents and offers sacrifice.

**Legacy**
- David remains the model king despite failures.

## Core Theological Takeaways
- God desires **obedience from the heart**, not mere внешнее actions.
- Rejecting God leads to **disorder in leadership and life**.
- David prefigures Christ as the **true King and shepherd**.
- God’s covenant promises are **faithful and enduring**.
- Sin has real consequences, even for the chosen.
- True repentance restores relationship with God.
