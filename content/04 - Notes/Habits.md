# Habits
---

Habits are the daily routine that we have, activities that are simply part of who we are. To build a habit, it just takes consistency.

Make a spreadsheet, write down habits to track, tick the boxes as long as you accomplished something related to that habit on that day, and see the streak form.

It takes a while to actually make a habit stick, I've read anywhere from 20 to 100 something days. It just takes time. Stage 1 is actually getting used to doing something. Stage 2 is doing that something to get good at it. But stage 1 is important and needs to happen first.

Make progress on the path of least resistance

> Standardize before you optimize

It will take time and you won't see a lot of progress at first, but when it starts to rain...it POURS.

To form a new habit: repeat the task consistently, and reward yourself afterwards so its strengthened by dopamine

To remove a habit: when the cue occurs have a replacement activity or habit
