# Privacy
---

Nowadays collecting information is much easier and there are thousands of databases out there

The fourth amendment protects the right to be secure against "unreasonable searches and seizures" and requires warrants based on "probable cause"

Is there a "right to be forgotten"?

## Notable Court Cases
The Supreme Court ruled in _Kyllo v. United States_ (2001) that thermal imaging of a home from the outside without a warrant was a search

The _United States v. Miller_ (1976) ruling stated that sharing information with banks means no reasonable expectation of privacy for that information exists
