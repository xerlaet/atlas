# Greedy Algorithms
---

A **Greedy Algorithm** will make the optimal choice at each step without considering consequences

In order for it to work two properties must be satisfied:

1. [[Greedy-Choice Property]] meaning the optimal solution can be started with a local choice
2. [[Optimal Substructure]] where each subproblem must also have an optimal solution

Proving the greedy-choice property can be difficult, often [[Proof by Contradiction]] or an exchange argument is a good choice

An alternative is to show that the problem can be represented as a [[Matroids|Matroid]]
