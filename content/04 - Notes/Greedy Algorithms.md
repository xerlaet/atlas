# Greedy Algorithms
---

A **Greedy Algorithm** will make the optimal choice at each step without considering consequences

In order for it to work two properties must be satisfied:

1. [Greedy-Choice Property](/notes/greedy-choice-property) meaning the optimal solution can be started with a local choice
2. [Optimal Substructure](/notes/optimal-substructure) where each subproblem must also have an optimal solution

Proving the greedy-choice property can be difficult, often [Proof by Contradiction](/notes/proof-by-contradiction) or an exchange argument is a good choice

An alternative is to show that the problem can be represented as a [Matroid](/notes/matroids)
