# Motivation
---


I've noticed that I no longer have the dedication that I used to for modeling. At Savio, I modeled all over my free time, it seems like every waking moment was spent thinking about robotics or finishing something as quickly as possible in order to work on robotics. Now I don't have that same obsession. Part of it might be that it's vex instead of ftc, but I think that there is more to the story than just that. Perhaps I am burned out from all of my work in the past couple years, especially last year. It could be the lessons that I learned from it, in how my college applications got wrecked because of it. I am lucky that I made it to UTD and got a $10,000 scholarship each year. If I had done just a little more robotics, I wouldn't have this. That scared me.

Another idea is that motivation is fleeting. it doesn't always stick around. [Habits](/notes/habits) are stronger and last.
