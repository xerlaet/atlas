# Bootstrapping
---

A statistical technique that uses repeated sampling from an existing sample to estimate how reliable a statistic is

Standard error, bias, confidence intervals

1. Population distribution: The distribution of individual observations in the real population. Usually unknown.
2. Sampling distribution: The distribution of a statistic across hypothetical repeated samples from that population. Usually unknown.
3. Bootstrap distribution: The distribution of a statistic across repeated samples drawn with replacement from our observed sample. We can simulate it.
Bootstrapping uses the third distribution to approximate the second, allowing us to estimate the uncertainty of a statistic without knowing the population distribution.
