# Intelligence
---

Definitions for **intelligence** are widely debated, but generally include some aspect of "doing the right thing" based on some objective, a property of internal thought processes and reasoning, or intelligent behavior. And human reasoning is definitely not perfect

Another definition is the ability to acquire knowledge (learn) and apply that knowledge (reason)
