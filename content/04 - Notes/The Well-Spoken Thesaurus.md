# The Well-Spoken Thesaurus
---

> Words are like little gods

The well-spoken few are viewed by others in a different way. More is expected of them, and that expectation is a self-fulfilling prophecy

Using too many large words out of place can make you sound academic and eccentric, the trick is to use appropriate language in a better way

## Lessons
Everyday words and simple replacements/omissions can be poetic

Replace "about" with "of": There is talk ~~about~~ **of** a takeover, but the would-be CEO knows nothing ~~about~~ **of** the publishing business

Switch up the figurative and literal

I remember once how I let myself fall in love; ~~now I'm more careful with my emotions~~ now I always work with a net
