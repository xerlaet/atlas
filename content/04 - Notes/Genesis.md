## 1-11: God & The World
**Creation & Purpose**
- God creates out of nothing (*ex nihilo*), bringing order, goodness, and beauty (Gen 1–2; CCC 296–299).
- Humans are made in God’s image to know, love, and steward creation (Gen 1:26–28; CCC 356–357).

**The Fall**
- The Tree = trust: accept God’s definition of good/evil or define it ourselves.
- The serpent tempts distrust: *“Does God really love you?”* (Gen 3).
- Sin enters → death, disorder, separation.

**Protoevangelium (First Gospel)**
- Gen 3:15: offspring of the woman will crush the serpent.
- Fulfilled in Christ; Mary as the New Eve (CCC 410–411).

**Spread of Sin**
- Cain kills Abel → violence escalates (Gen 4).
- Humanity corrupts itself despite God’s patience.

**Flood (New Creation)**
- Noah = new Adam.
- Flood = judgment + cleansing; prefigures Baptism (1 Pet 3:20–21; CCC 1219).

**Tower of Babel**
- Prideful self-exaltation → God scatters nations (Gen 11).
- Unity apart from God leads to division.

## 12-50: God & Abraham’s Family
**Abrahamic Covenant**
- Promise: land, descendants, blessing to all nations (Gen 12:1–3).
- Faith, not perfection, is key (CCC 145–146).

**Abraham’s Failures**
- Lies about Sarah; takes Hagar.
- God remains faithful despite human weakness.

**Jacob (Israel)**
- Deceiver → transformed by encounter with God.
- Wrestles with God → renamed *Israel* (“struggles with God”) (Gen 32).

**Joseph**
- Betrayed → exalted in Egypt.
- Saves many during famine.
- “God meant it for good” (Gen 50:20).
- Prefigures Christ.

**Judah’s Prophecy**
- Gen 49:10: ruler from Judah → obedience of nations.
- Fulfilled in Christ.

## Core Theological Takeaways
- Creation is **good**, not evil (CCC 299).
- Man is created for **communion with God** (CCC 1).
- Sin = **distrust of God**.
- God’s plan is **faithful despite human failure**.
- Genesis points to **Christ and redemption**.
