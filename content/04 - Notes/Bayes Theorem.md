# Bayes Theorem
---

With the fact that
$$
P(A\mid B)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}=\frac{P(B\mid A)P(A)}{P(B)}
$$
The following probability holds true
$$
P(A_{i}\mid B)=\frac
{P(B\mid A_{i})P(A_{i})}
{\sum_{j}P(B\mid A_{j})P(A_{j})}
=\frac{\text{probability of the result}}{\text{every way to get the result}}
$$
